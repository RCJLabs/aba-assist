---
id: most-to-least-prompting
term: Most-to-Least Prompting
aliases: [MTL, decreasing assistance]
abbreviation: MTL
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A prompting procedure that starts with the most intrusive prompt needed to
    guarantee the correct response and moves to less intrusive prompts across trials
    as the learner succeeds. It minimizes errors early on and suits new skills and
    learners who make many errors, at the cost of possibly giving more help than needed.
  plain: >-
    Start with a lot of help so the learner gets it right, then give less and less
    help as they succeed. Good for brand-new skills.
  gloss: Start with full help and fade downward
examples:
  - text: >-
      Hand-over-hand for the first sessions of a tracing task, then a light touch at the
      wrist, then a gesture, then nothing.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Waiting for the learner to try alone and adding help only if they fail. That is
      least-to-most.
    setting: clinic
