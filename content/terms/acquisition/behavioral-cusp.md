id: behavioral-cusp
term: Behavioral Cusp
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A behavior change that opens the person's world to new contingencies, reinforcers,
    environments and further learning that were previously out of reach — learning to
    read, to walk, to ask questions. Cusps are prioritized as goals because their
    effects go far beyond the behavior itself.
  plain: >-
    A skill that unlocks many other skills and experiences. Learning to read is one:
    after it, the person can learn from every sign and book.
  gloss: A skill that opens the door to much more learning
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who begins to imitate can now be taught almost anything by demonstration.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Learning to name one more color. Useful, but it does not by itself open up new
      environments.
    setting: clinic
