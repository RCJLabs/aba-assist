term: Group Contingency
aliases:

category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A contingency in which a consequence for a group depends on the behavior of some
    or all of its members. Independent: each member earns for their own behavior.
    Dependent: the whole group earns based on one member or subgroup. Interdependent:
    the whole group earns only if every member, or the group as a whole, meets the
    criterion. Each carries a different risk of peer pressure.
  plain: >-
    A reward for a group that depends on how members behave. It can depend on each
    person alone, on one person, or on everyone together.
  gloss: A group consequence based on members' behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      The class earns extra recess if the whole class stays under five call-outs for
      the day: an interdependent group contingency.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Each student earns their own sticker for their own quiet work with no shared
      outcome at all. With nothing shared, it is an individual contingency.
    setting: school
