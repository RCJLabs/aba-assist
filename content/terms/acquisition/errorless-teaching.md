id: errorless-teaching
term: Errorless Teaching
aliases: [errorless learning, errorless discrimination training]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Teaching arranged so the learner makes few or no errors, usually by prompting the
    correct response immediately and fading the prompt gradually. It rests on the
    finding that errors are not needed for learning a discrimination and that
    repeated errors can produce frustration and their own stimulus control.
  plain: >-
    Teaching set up so the learner almost never gets it wrong. Help comes right away
    and is slowly removed. Mistakes are avoided rather than corrected.
  gloss: Teaching arranged to prevent errors
examples:
  - text: >-
      A new word is taught by saying it immediately after showing the card, then
      waiting a little longer each session before saying it.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Letting the learner guess and correcting each wrong guess. That relies on error
      correction.
    setting: clinic
