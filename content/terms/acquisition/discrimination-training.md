id: discrimination-training
term: Discrimination Training
aliases: [simple discrimination, conditional discrimination]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Teaching a learner to respond to one stimulus and not to others by reinforcing the
    response in the presence of the target and withholding reinforcement in the presence
    of the rest. In a simple discrimination one stimulus signals reinforcement; in a
    conditional discrimination the correct choice depends on a sample or instruction,
    as in matching-to-sample or receptive identification.
  plain: >-
    Teaching a learner to respond to the right thing and not the others. Sometimes which
    thing is right depends on what was asked.
  gloss: Teaching a learner to respond only to the correct stimulus
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner is reinforced for touching the spoon when asked for a spoon and not
      when they touch the fork.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Reinforcing every touch of any item on the table. No discrimination is being
      taught.
    setting: clinic
