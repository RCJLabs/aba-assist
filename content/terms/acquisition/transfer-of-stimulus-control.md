id: transfer-of-stimulus-control
term: Transfer of Stimulus Control
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    The shift of control over a response from a prompt to the natural stimulus that is
    supposed to occasion it. Every prompting procedure exists to bring this about, and
    it is complete when the learner responds correctly to the natural cue with no prompt
    at all. It is also the process by which, for example, a mand prompted by an echoic
    model comes under the control of the motivating operation alone.
  plain: >-
    The moment the learner responds to the real cue instead of to the help. It is the
    goal of all prompting.
  gloss: Control shifts from the prompt to the natural cue
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who used to need a pointed finger now touches the correct card on the
      instruction alone.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who still waits for the pointed finger every time. Control remains with
      the prompt.
    setting: clinic
