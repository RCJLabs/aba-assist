id: teaching-loosely
term: Teaching Loosely
aliases: [train loosely, loose training]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Deliberately varying the non-essential features of teaching — wording, tone,
    position, materials, time of day — so that no irrelevant detail comes to control
    the response. It is one of the generalization strategies described by Stokes and
    Baer and it counters the tendency for a skill to work only under the exact
    conditions of teaching.
  plain: >-
    Change the small things that do not matter, like your wording or where you sit, so
    the learner does not get stuck on them.
  gloss: Varying irrelevant details so they do not control the response
examples:
  - text: >-
      The instruction for a matching task is given sometimes as "match," sometimes as
      "put with same," sometimes with a gesture, from different chairs.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Always saying "match" in the same tone from the same chair with the same cards.
      Tightly controlled teaching, which risks a narrow skill.
    setting: clinic
