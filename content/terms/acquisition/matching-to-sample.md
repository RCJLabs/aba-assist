id: matching-to-sample
term: Matching-to-Sample
aliases: [MTS, match to sample]
abbreviation: MTS
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A conditional-discrimination procedure in which a sample stimulus is presented,
    followed by an array of comparison stimuli, and the learner is reinforced for
    selecting the comparison that goes with the sample. The relation may be identity
    (same object), or arbitrary (a word to a picture), and it is the standard format
    for teaching and testing stimulus equivalence.
  plain: >-
    Show one item, then show several choices, and the learner picks the one that goes
    with it. Used to teach matching, and later, meaning.
  gloss: Choose the comparison that goes with the sample
examples:
  - text: >-
      Shown a picture of a dog, the learner picks the printed word "dog" from three
      words.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asked "touch dog" with no sample shown. The instruction alone controls the
      response; that is listener responding.
    setting: school
