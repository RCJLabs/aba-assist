id: error-correction
term: Error Correction
aliases: [correction procedure, error-correction procedure]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A planned response to an incorrect answer, written into the program: typically the
    trial is re-presented with a prompt so the correct response occurs, and then
    re-presented again with less help. What counts as an error, how to respond and
    how many times to repeat are decided by the supervisor, and technicians follow the
    written version rather than improvising.
  plain: >-
    What to do when the learner gets it wrong, as written in the plan. Usually the
    question is asked again with help, then again with less help.
  gloss: The written procedure for responding to a wrong answer
examples:
  - text: >-
      The learner points to the wrong card. The technician re-presents the cards,
      points to the correct one, and then re-presents without pointing.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Prompting the right answer immediately so no error happens. That is errorless
      teaching.
    setting: clinic
