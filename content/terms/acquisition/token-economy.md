id: token-economy
term: Token Economy
aliases: [token system, point system]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A system in which target behaviors earn tokens — points, stars, chips — that can
    later be exchanged for backup reinforcers. The tokens become generalized conditioned
    reinforcers through the exchange. A working system specifies the behaviors, the
    token value, the backups and the exchange schedule, and is thinned over time.
  plain: >-
    Earning points or tokens for good behavior and trading them in later for things
    the person wants. It works because the tokens can be traded for many things.
  gloss: Tokens earned for behavior and exchanged for rewards
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner earns a star for each completed task and trades five stars for ten
      minutes on the tablet.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Handing out stars that can never be traded for anything. Without backup
      reinforcers, the stars have no reason to work.
    setting: school
