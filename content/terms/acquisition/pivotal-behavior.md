id: pivotal-behavior
term: Pivotal Behavior
aliases: [pivotal response]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A behavior that, once learned, produces improvements in other, untrained behaviors
    without those being taught directly — motivation to respond, self-initiation,
    responding to multiple cues. It differs from a cusp in that the payoff is
    collateral change in other behaviors rather than access to new environments.
  plain: >-
    A skill that, once learned, makes other skills improve on their own. Starting
    conversations is one: it leads to more language without more teaching.
  gloss: A behavior whose learning improves other behaviors untaught
examples:
  - text: >-
      After being taught to ask "what's that?", a learner's vocabulary grows from the
      answers without any further word-by-word teaching.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Learning to walk, which opens new places and contingencies. That is a cusp.
    setting: any
contrastWith: [behavioral-cusp]
