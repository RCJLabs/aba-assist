id: modeling
term: Modeling
aliases: [demonstration, video modeling, model prompt]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Demonstrating a behavior so the learner can imitate it. It works only for a learner
    who already imitates, it is more effective when the model is reinforced and the
    learner attends, and video modeling — a recorded demonstration watched before or
    during practice — is a widely used variation.
  plain: >-
    Showing the learner what to do so they can copy it. Only works if the learner can
    already imitate. Videos can do the showing.
  gloss: Demonstrating a behavior for the learner to copy
examples:
  - text: >-
      The technician shows how to stack three blocks, then hands over the blocks.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Telling the learner the steps in words. That is an instruction, not a model.
    setting: clinic
