id: least-to-most-prompting
term: Least-to-Most Prompting
aliases: [LTM, increasing assistance, system of least prompts]
abbreviation: LTM
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A prompting procedure that gives the learner a chance to respond on their own,
    then adds progressively more intrusive prompts only as needed within the trial.
    It gives the least help necessary and suits skills the learner partly has, at the
    cost of allowing more errors.
  plain: >-
    Let the learner try first. If they cannot do it, add a little help, then a little
    more, until they get it. Good for skills they partly have.
  gloss: Start with no help and add more only as needed
examples:
  - text: >-
      "Hang up your coat." Wait. Point at the hook. Wait. Model it. Wait. Guide the arm.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Guiding the arm from the first trial and reducing help over days. That is
      most-to-least.
    setting: school
