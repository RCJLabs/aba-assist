id: pairing
term: Pairing
aliases: [rapport building, conditioning the instructor as a reinforcer]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Repeatedly presenting a neutral stimulus — often the technician themselves, or a
    new activity — together with established reinforcers so that it acquires
    reinforcing value. In practice it is the early-session work of delivering
    preferred items and play freely so the learner approaches the person and the
    setting.
  plain: >-
    Making yourself, or a new thing, fun by showing up together with things the learner
    already loves. It is how a new helper becomes someone the learner wants to be with.
  gloss: Giving a neutral thing value by pairing it with reinforcers
examples:
  - text: >-
      For the first sessions, the technician hands over favorite toys and joins in play
      with no demands, until the learner starts approaching them.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Starting demands on the first day with an unfamiliar adult. Nothing has been
      paired yet.
    setting: clinic
