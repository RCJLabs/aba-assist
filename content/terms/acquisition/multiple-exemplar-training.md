term: Multiple Exemplar Training
aliases: [training sufficient exemplars, general case instruction]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Teaching a skill with many different examples of the stimuli, settings, people and
    materials the learner will meet, so that responding generalizes to untrained cases.
    It is the most reliable of the strategies for promoting generalization described
    by Stokes and Baer, and the alternative to hoping a skill taught with one example
    will spread on its own.
  plain: >-
    Teach the skill with many different examples, people and places. Then it works
    everywhere, not just with the teaching materials.
  gloss: Teaching with many examples so the skill generalizes
examples:
  - text: >-
      "Cup" is taught with six cups of different colors, sizes and materials, across
      the kitchen and the clinic.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Teaching "cup" with one blue cup at one table and expecting the word to spread.
    setting: clinic
