id: total-task-chaining
term: Total-Task Chaining
aliases: [whole-task chaining, total task presentation]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A chaining method in which the learner performs every step of the task analysis on
    every trial, with prompts given at whichever steps need them and faded step by step.
    It suits learners who already have some of the steps and it keeps the task whole,
    at the cost of longer trials.
  plain: >-
    The learner does the whole task every time, with help only on the steps they
    cannot do yet. Good when they already know some of the steps.
  gloss: Every step attempted each trial, with help where needed
examples:
  - text: >-
      Making a sandwich: the learner does all eight steps each time; the technician
      helps with spreading, the one step the learner has not mastered.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Teaching only the first step and doing the rest for the learner. That is forward
      chaining.
    setting: home
contrastWith: [forward-chaining]
