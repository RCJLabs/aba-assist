id: schedule-thinning
term: Schedule Thinning
aliases: [thinning reinforcement, fading reinforcement]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Gradually reducing how often a behavior is reinforced — from every response toward
    an intermittent schedule closer to what the natural environment provides — while
    keeping the behavior going. Thinning too fast produces ratio strain and a collapse
    in responding; the pace is set by the data.
  plain: >-
    Slowly giving rewards less often once a skill is learned, so it keeps going without
    constant rewards. Going too fast makes the behavior fall apart.
  gloss: Gradually reinforcing less often
examples:
  - text: >-
      Praise for every correct answer, then every second, then about every fourth, over
      several weeks.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Dropping from every answer to every tenth answer in one day. The behavior
      collapses, which is ratio strain.
    setting: clinic
