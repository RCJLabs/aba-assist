id: prompt
term: Prompt
aliases: [response prompt, stimulus prompt, assistance, cue]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Extra help given before or during a response so that the correct response occurs
    and can be reinforced. Response prompts act on the learner — a spoken hint, a
    gesture, a model, physical guidance. Stimulus prompts change the teaching materials
    — a highlighted answer, an enlarged target. All prompts are temporary and must be
    faded so the natural cue takes over.
  plain: >-
    Help that makes the right answer happen so it can be rewarded. It can be a hint,
    a gesture, a demonstration, or a change to the materials. It is always meant to be
    removed later.
  gloss: Temporary help that makes the correct response occur
examples:
  - text: >-
      After "touch red," the technician points to the red card and the learner touches it.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The instruction "touch red" itself. That is the natural cue the learner is meant
      to respond to, not extra help.
    setting: clinic
