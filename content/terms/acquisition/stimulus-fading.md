id: stimulus-fading
term: Stimulus Fading
aliases: [within-stimulus prompt fading]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    Gradually changing a physical feature of the teaching stimulus — size, color,
    position, intensity — that was added to make the correct response likely, until
    the stimulus is in its natural form. Stimulus shaping is the related method of
    gradually changing the shape of the stimulus itself.
  plain: >-
    Making the right answer stand out at first, such as bigger or brighter, then slowly
    making it look normal again.
  gloss: Gradually removing a built-in feature that made the answer obvious
examples:
  - text: >-
      The correct picture starts out three times larger than the others and shrinks a
      little each session.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Waiting a little longer before pointing to the correct picture. Delaying a
      response prompt is time delay.
    setting: clinic
