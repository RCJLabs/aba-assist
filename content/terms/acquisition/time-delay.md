id: time-delay
term: Time Delay
aliases: [progressive time delay, constant time delay, prompt delay]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A prompt-fading method in which the prompt is given at the same moment as the
    natural cue at first, and then delayed by a growing interval (progressive) or a
    fixed interval (constant) so the learner has a chance to respond before the prompt
    arrives. Responding before the prompt shows stimulus control has transferred to the
    natural cue.
  plain: >-
    At first the help comes right away. Then the helper waits a little longer each time
    before helping. When the learner answers before the help, the skill has transferred.
  gloss: Fading prompts by waiting longer before giving them
examples:
  - text: >-
      "What is this?" — immediately "ball." Next session, a two-second wait before
      "ball." Later, four seconds, until the learner answers first.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Making the correct card larger and gradually shrinking it. That changes the
      materials, which is stimulus fading.
    setting: clinic
