id: incidental-teaching
term: Incidental Teaching
aliases: [naturalistic language teaching]
category: acquisition
definition:
  technical: >-
    A naturalistic teaching method in which the adult arranges the environment so the
    learner shows interest in something, then uses that moment to prompt a more
    elaborate response before providing it. The learner's own initiation is the
    starting point, which builds in motivation and promotes generalization.
  plain: >-
    Wait until the learner wants something, then use that moment to teach a better way
    to ask before giving it. The learner starts it, which is why it works.
  gloss: Teaching in the moment a learner shows interest
examples:
  - text: >-
      A child reaches for a truck on a high shelf. The adult prompts "truck, please"
      and hands it over when the child says it.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Sitting at a table running ten trials of "say truck." That is discrete-trial
      teaching.
    setting: clinic
