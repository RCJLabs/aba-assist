---
id: parsimony
term: Parsimony
aliases: [principle of parsimony, Occam's razor]
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    The rule that simpler explanations are tried and ruled out before more complex or
    abstract ones are accepted. In behavior analysis it steers explanation toward
    observable environmental variables before invoking hidden mental causes.
  plain: >-
    Try the simple explanation first. Only reach for a complex one after the simple one
    has failed.
  gloss: Prefer the simplest explanation that fits the data
examples:
  - text: >-
      Before concluding a learner "has anxiety about work," the analyst checks whether
      the tasks are simply followed by escape.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Assuming a complicated inner cause when the data show the behavior only happens
      when attention is withdrawn.
    setting: home
