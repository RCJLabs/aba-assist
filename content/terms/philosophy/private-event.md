id: private-event
term: Private Event
aliases: [covert behavior, private behavior]
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    Behavior that only the person behaving can observe directly, such as thinking,
    feeling pain, or imagining. In radical behaviorism these are part of the subject
    matter, governed by the same principles as public behavior, and they are not treated
    as the causes of public behavior.
  plain: >-
    Things a person does that nobody else can see, like thinking or feeling. They are
    still behavior, just hidden.
  gloss: Behavior only the person doing it can observe
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner silently rehearses the steps of a routine before starting. The rehearsal
      is behavior, even though it is not visible to staff.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner quietly humming. Quiet is not the same as private; another person can
      still hear it.
    setting: home
