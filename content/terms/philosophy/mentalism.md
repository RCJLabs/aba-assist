id: mentalism
term: Mentalism
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    An approach that explains behavior by appeal to an inner mental dimension — a mind,
    a will, a drive — that is said to cause it. Behavior analysis rejects this because
    the inner cause is inferred from the very behavior it is supposed to explain, so the
    account adds nothing that could be tested or changed.
  plain: >-
    Explaining behavior by pointing to something inside, like willpower or a bad
    attitude. It sounds like an explanation but gives you nothing to work with.
  gloss: Explaining behavior by an inner cause that cannot be tested
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student is said to refuse work because he lacks motivation, and the lack of
      motivation is inferred from the refusal.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Noting that refusal is followed by removal of the task and testing whether that
      removal maintains it. That is a functional account.
    setting: school
