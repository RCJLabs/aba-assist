aliases: [EAB, basic research]

category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    The basic-research branch of behavior analysis. It studies the principles of behavior
    themselves, often under highly controlled laboratory conditions and with nonhuman
    subjects, and is not concerned with whether a particular behavior is important to
    anyone's life.
  plain: >-
    The lab side of the science. It studies how behavior works in general, not how to
    help one person.
  gloss: The basic-research branch of behavior analysis

      important change, which makes this applied work.
