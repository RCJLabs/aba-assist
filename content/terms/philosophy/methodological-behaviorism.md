id: methodological-behaviorism
term: Methodological Behaviorism
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    A position that restricts the science of behavior to publicly observable events,
    treating thoughts and feelings as outside its scope rather than as behavior to be
    analyzed. Radical behaviorism rejected this restriction.
  plain: >-
    The older view that only what can be seen from outside counts for science. It leaves
    thoughts and feelings out entirely.
  gloss: Only public behavior counts; private events are excluded
examples:
  - text: >-
      A researcher who declines to study covert self-talk because it cannot be observed
      by a second person is taking a methodological position.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Analyzing a learner's report of pain as a verbal response with its own controlling
      variables. That treats a private event as behavior, which is the radical view.
    setting: clinic
