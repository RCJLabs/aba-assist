id: radical-behaviorism
term: Radical Behaviorism
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    Skinner's philosophy of the science of behavior. It treats private events such as
    thoughts and feelings as behavior subject to the same principles as public behavior,
    rather than as causes standing outside the analysis, and it seeks explanation in
    the person's environment and history rather than in a separate mental realm.
  plain: >-
    The view behind ABA. Thoughts and feelings are real, but they are behavior too, and
    they do not explain other behavior by themselves.
  gloss: Skinner's philosophy; private events count as behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner says they feel nervous before a task. A radical behaviorist takes the
      report seriously and asks what in the situation produces both the feeling and the
      avoidance.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Saying the avoidance happens because of the nervousness and stopping there. That
      uses one behavior to explain another without looking at the environment.
    setting: clinic
