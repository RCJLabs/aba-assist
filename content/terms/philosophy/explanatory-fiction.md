---
id: explanatory-fiction
term: Explanatory Fiction
aliases: [circular explanation]
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    A supposed cause that is nothing more than a restatement of the behavior it is
    meant to explain, so the explanation goes in a circle. "He hits because he is
    aggressive" names the behavior twice and explains it once, which is to say not at all.
  plain: >-
    A fake explanation that just describes the behavior with a new word. It feels like an
    answer but it is the question again.
  gloss: A circular explanation that renames the behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner does not finish tasks. Staff say it is because of poor focus. Asked how
      they know focus is poor, they point to the unfinished tasks.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Saying tasks go unfinished because the learner leaves the table whenever a
      demand is placed and the demand is then dropped. That names an outside variable.
    setting: school
