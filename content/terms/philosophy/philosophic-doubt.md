id: philosophic-doubt
term: Philosophic Doubt
aliases: [scientific skepticism]
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    The habit of treating every finding, including one's own, as provisional and open to
    revision by new evidence. It keeps a science from hardening into dogma and keeps a
    practitioner checking whether a favored procedure still works.
  plain: >-
    Keep questioning, even things you are sure about. New data can always change the
    answer.
  gloss: Treat every conclusion as open to revision
examples:
  - text: >-
      An analyst re-checks whether a long-running plan is still effective rather than
      assuming it is because it worked last year.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Dismissing a new study because it contradicts what the team already believes.
    setting: any
