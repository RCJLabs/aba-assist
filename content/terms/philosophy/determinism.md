---
id: determinism
term: Determinism
aliases: [lawfulness of behavior]
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    The assumption that behavior is lawful: it has causes in the environment and in the
    person's history, and those causes can in principle be discovered. Without it there
    would be nothing for a science of behavior to find.
  plain: >-
    The idea that behavior happens for reasons, and the reasons can be found. This is the
    starting point for studying behavior at all.
  gloss: Behavior has causes that can be discovered
examples:
  - text: >-
      An analyst assumes a learner's outburst has an environmental cause and looks for
      it, instead of concluding the learner is simply unpredictable.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Explaining a behavior as "just who he is." That treats the behavior as uncaused
      and stops the search for what maintains it.
    setting: home
