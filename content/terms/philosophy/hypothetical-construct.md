id: hypothetical-construct
term: Hypothetical Construct
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    An entity or process that is presumed to exist but has not been observed, invoked to
    explain behavior. Constructs are not forbidden in science, but behavior analysis is
    wary of them because they are easily mistaken for causes and cannot be manipulated
    directly.
  plain: >-
    Something assumed to exist inside a person to explain what they do, even though no
    one has seen it. It cannot be changed directly.
  gloss: An unobserved inner process assumed to explain behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      Describing a learner's difficulty as "low working memory capacity" invokes a
      construct rather than something a program can act on.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Describing the same difficulty as errors that rise when instructions have more
      than two steps. That is an observation, and it suggests what to change.
    setting: school
