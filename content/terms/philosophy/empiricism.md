id: empiricism
term: Empiricism
category: philosophy
definition:
  technical: >-
    The commitment to objective observation and measurement as the basis for knowledge
    claims. In practice it means decisions rest on data that were actually collected,
    not on impressions, tradition, or authority.
  plain: >-
    Trusting what you can observe and measure over what you assume. Data decide, not
    opinions.
  gloss: Knowledge comes from observation and measurement
examples:
  - text: >-
      A team keeps a program because the graph shows the behavior falling, even though
      one staff member feels it is not working.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Keeping a procedure because the agency has always used it, without ever checking
      whether it changes anything.
    setting: any
