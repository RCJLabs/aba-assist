term: Pyramidal Training
aliases: [train-the-trainer, tiered training]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A staff training model in which a supervisor trains a small number of people to
    criterion and those people then train others, with the supervisor monitoring what
    the second tier delivers. It scales training past what one supervisor can deliver
    directly, and its known failure is drift: the second tier teaches what it remembers
    unless it is monitored against the same criteria.
  plain: >-
    Train a few people properly, then have them train the rest. It only works if you
    keep checking what they teach.
  gloss: Training a first tier of staff who then train the rest
examples:
  - text: >-
      Two senior technicians are trained to criterion on a prompting procedure and each
      trains three colleagues, with the supervisor scoring the results.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asking an experienced colleague to show a new starter the job, with nobody
      checking what was shown.
    setting: clinic
