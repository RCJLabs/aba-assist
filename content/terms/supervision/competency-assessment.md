category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A structured, in-person demonstration that a person can perform a set of required
    skills to criterion, observed and signed off by a qualified assessor. For
    technicians, an initial competency assessment is required for certification after
    the 40-hour training, and a recertification assessment has been required for
    renewal; the 2026 changes move maintenance toward professional development instead.
  plain: >-
    Showing an assessor, in person, that you can actually do the required skills. It is
    part of getting certified as a technician.
  gloss: In-person demonstration of required skills to an assessor
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician demonstrates a preference assessment and a discrete-trial program
      with a client while the assessor scores each task.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Passing the written certification exam. That tests knowledge; the competency
      assessment tests performance.
    setting: any
