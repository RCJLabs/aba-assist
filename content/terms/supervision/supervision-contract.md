id: supervision-contract
term: Supervision Contract
aliases: [supervision agreement]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A written agreement between supervisor and supervisee setting out what each will do:
    frequency and format of contacts, how performance will be assessed, how feedback is
    given and received, and how problems are raised. Expectations for the supervisor are
    as load-bearing as those for the supervisee, because a supervisee cannot meet a
    standard that assumed observations and answers that never arrived.
  plain: >-
    A written deal about how supervision will work. It says what the supervisor will do
    as well as what the supervisee will do.
  gloss: A written agreement setting out what supervision will involve
examples:
  - text: >-
      The agreement states two observations a month, written feedback within three days,
      and how the supervisee raises a concern about a plan.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A verbal understanding that the pair will meet when they can, with no statement of
      what each owes the other.
    setting: clinic
