id: in-situ-training
term: In-Situ Training
aliases: [on-the-job training, in situ feedback]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    Training delivered in the setting and conditions where the skill will be used, with
    feedback given there. It is the component most often missing when staff reach
    criterion in role-play and then perform poorly with a client, because a skill
    trained only in a quiet room is a skill under the control of a quiet room.
  plain: >-
    Training people in the real setting, not just in a meeting room. Skills practiced in
    a quiet room often do not survive a real session.
  gloss: Training and feedback given where the skill will be used
examples:
  - text: >-
      After role-play, the supervisor observes the first two real sessions and gives
      feedback between them.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Signing a staff member off on the basis of a role-play performance in the office.
    setting: clinic
