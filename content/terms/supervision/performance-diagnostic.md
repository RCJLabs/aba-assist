term: Performance Diagnostic
aliases: [performance analysis, can't-do versus won't-do, PDC]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A function-based assessment of why a staff member is not performing as expected,
    before a remedy is chosen. It distinguishes skill deficits (the person cannot do it)
    from problems of antecedents (unclear expectations, missing materials), of
    consequences (no reinforcement, competing contingencies) and of resources. Training
    fixes only the first; the others need different solutions.
  plain: >-
    Working out why a staff member is not doing the job right before deciding what to
    do about it. Sometimes they cannot; sometimes nothing supports doing it.
  gloss: Finding out why staff performance falls short before fixing it
examples:
  - text: >-
      Data sheets keep going unfinished. The supervisor finds the sheets are stored in
      another room, and moves them; no retraining was needed.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Scheduling a retraining for every performance problem without checking whether
      the person already knows how.
    setting: any
