id: reciprocal-feedback
term: Reciprocal Feedback
aliases: [two-way feedback]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A supervisory arrangement in which feedback travels in both directions: the
    supervisor invites it from the supervisee and acts on it. It is worth building
    deliberately because the supervisee sees what the supervisor cannot, including which
    of the supervisor's own instructions are unworkable in the setting.
  plain: >-
    Feedback going both ways. The supervisee sees things the supervisor does not, so
    asking them is useful.
  gloss: Feedback that travels from supervisee to supervisor too
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor ends each observation by asking what got in the way, and changes a
      step in response.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor who invites comments and never changes anything after receiving them.
    setting: clinic
