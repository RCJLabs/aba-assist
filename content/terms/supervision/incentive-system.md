id: incentive-system
term: Incentive System
aliases: [staff incentives, performance pay]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    An arrangement delivering consequences for staff performance beyond the ordinary
    contingencies of the job — recognition, preferred assignments, time, money. It is
    the component of performance management most likely to misfire, because an incentive
    attached to a measure rather than to the performance reliably produces the measure.
  plain: >-
    Rewards for staff performance. Attach one to a number instead of the work and you
    will get the number.
  gloss: Consequences arranged for staff performance at work
examples:
  - text: >-
      Recognition is given for integrity scores verified by observation rather than for
      the count of sessions logged.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Paying a bonus on session volume, which is met by running more sessions rather
      than better ones.
