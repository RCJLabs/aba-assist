id: performance-feedback
term: Performance Feedback
aliases: [feedback, corrective feedback, positive feedback]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    Information given to a person about their performance, used to improve it. The
    forms with the best evidence are specific, delivered soon after the performance,
    combine what was done well with what to change and how, and are followed by a
    check that the change happened. Feedback is the core of behavioral supervision and
    a required activity in the supervision of technicians.
  plain: >-
    Telling someone clearly what they did well and what to do differently, soon after,
    and checking later that it changed. The heart of good supervision.
  gloss: Specific, timely information about performance to improve it
examples:
  - text: >-
      Right after a session, the supervisor says the prompts were on time, asks for a
      full three-second wait before the second prompt next time, and says they will
      watch for it on Thursday.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      An annual review that says "good job overall." Too late and too vague to change
      anything specific.
    setting: any
