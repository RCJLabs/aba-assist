term: Staff Turnover
aliases: [attrition]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    The rate at which staff leave and are replaced, which in behaviour-analytic services
    is both an outcome of supervision quality and a cause of poor client outcomes. Every
    departure resets a learner's relationships and the team's procedural integrity,
    which is why turnover belongs in a supervisor's data rather than only in a
    manager's.
  plain: >-
    How often staff leave and are replaced. Each departure costs the learner
    relationships and costs the team consistency.
  gloss: The rate at which staff leave, and what that costs clients
examples:
  - text: >-
      A service tracks how long technicians stay alongside its integrity scores, and
      finds the two move together.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Treating departures as a recruitment problem with no connection to how people are
      supervised.
    setting: clinic
