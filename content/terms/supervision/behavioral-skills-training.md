term: Behavioral Skills Training
aliases: [BST]
abbreviation: BST
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A training package for teaching a skill to staff, caregivers or learners: explain
    the skill and why it matters, model it, have the trainee rehearse it, and give
    feedback, repeating rehearsal and feedback until the trainee meets a performance
    criterion. It is the best-supported method for training people to implement
    procedures correctly, and telling alone is its weakest component.
  plain: >-
    Teach a skill by explaining it, showing it, having the person practice it, and
    giving feedback until they can do it. Just telling people is not enough.
  gloss: Instruction, modeling, rehearsal and feedback to criterion
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor explains a prompting procedure, demonstrates it with a colleague,
      has the technician practice in role play, and gives feedback until it is right.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Handing a technician the written protocol and asking them to read it. That is
      instruction only.
    setting: clinic
