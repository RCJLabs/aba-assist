term: Supervision
aliases: [ongoing supervision, RBT supervision, clinical supervision]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    The ongoing oversight of a practitioner's work by a qualified supervisor who is
    responsible for it. For technicians it is a certification requirement with fixed
    minimums — a percentage of service hours each month, a minimum number of real-time
    contacts including direct observation with a client, and at least one individual
    contact — and it must be documented and kept for years. Its purpose is to build
    the technician's clinical, professional and ethical skills and to protect clients.
  plain: >-
    A qualified person watches your work often, gives feedback, and is responsible for
    it. Technicians must have it to stay certified. There are set monthly minimums.
  gloss: Required ongoing oversight of a practitioner's work
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor observes a technician running a program with a client, gives
      feedback afterward, and records the date, length and format.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A monthly staff meeting about scheduling. With no client focus and no feedback on
      the technician's work, it does not count as supervision.
    setting: any
