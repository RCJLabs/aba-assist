term: Individual Supervision
aliases: [one-to-one supervision]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    Supervision delivered to one supervisee at a time, which allows observation of that
    person's own performance and feedback specific to it. Certification requirements
    typically set a minimum proportion of supervision that must take this form, because
    the group format cannot deliver individual observation.
  plain: >-
    Supervision with one person at a time, so the feedback is about what they actually
    did.
  gloss: Supervision of one supervisee at a time
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor observes one technician with a client and debriefs with them alone
      afterwards.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A meeting with four supervisees where each takes a turn describing a case.
    setting: clinic
