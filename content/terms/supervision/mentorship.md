id: mentorship
term: Mentorship
aliases: [mentoring]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A developmental relationship aimed at a person's longer-term growth in the
    profession, distinct from the supervision that carries responsibility for their
    current work. A supervisee may need expertise their supervisor does not have, and
    seeking it openly is appropriate — the supervisor still holds responsibility for the
    work and needs to know what is being learned and applied.
  plain: >-
    A longer-term relationship about growing in the profession. It is not the same as
    the supervision that signs off your work.
  gloss: A development relationship distinct from responsible supervision
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisee arranges consultation with a specialist in an area their supervisor
      does not practice, with the supervisor's knowledge.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Learning a new specialism privately and beginning to apply it to cases without
      telling the supervisor.
    setting: clinic
