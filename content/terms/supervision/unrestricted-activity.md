term: Unrestricted Activity
aliases: [unrestricted hours, restricted activity, fieldwork activities]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    In supervised fieldwork toward analyst certification, an activity that resembles
    what a certified analyst does — observing and analyzing data, designing programs,
    training staff and caregivers, meeting with clients about services — as opposed to
    restricted activities, which are the direct implementation of existing programs
    with clients. A majority of fieldwork hours must be unrestricted.
  plain: >-
    Fieldwork tasks that are analyst work, like designing programs and training staff,
    rather than delivering sessions. Most of a trainee's hours have to be this kind.
  gloss: Fieldwork that is analyst-level work, not direct delivery
examples:
  - text: >-
      A trainee graphs a client's data, drafts a revised program, and reviews it with
      their supervisor.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Running a discrete-trial session as written. That is restricted activity.
    setting: clinic
