id: remediation-plan
term: Remediation Plan
aliases: [performance improvement plan]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    A written plan addressing a supervisee's performance shortfall: what the gap is in
    observable terms, what will be done about it, what standard closes it, and by when.
    It follows a diagnosis of why the performance is short — a plan that prescribes
    training for a problem of competing contingencies changes nothing and costs the
    relationship.
  plain: >-
    A written plan for fixing a performance problem. It should follow from working out
    the cause, not start with more training.
  gloss: A written plan for closing a supervisee performance gap
examples:
  - text: >-
      Observation shows the step is skipped only on late shifts, so the plan changes the
      late-shift setup rather than repeating training.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Assigning a refresher course to somebody who performs the step correctly whenever
      they are observed.
    setting: clinic
