term: Professional Development Unit
aliases: [PDU, RBT professional development]
abbreviation: PDU
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    The unit in which technicians' ongoing professional development is counted for
    recertification under the 2026 requirements: one hour of qualifying activity is
    one unit, a fixed number is required per two-year cycle, and qualifying activities
    include an organization's behavior-analytic in-service training, events from
    authorized continuing-education providers, and university coursework. Ongoing
    supervision and client-specific training do not count.
  plain: >-
    The credit technicians earn for training that expands their skills, needed to
    recertify every two years from 2027. Supervision hours do not count toward it.
  gloss: Credit for technician professional development
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician attends a two-hour agency in-service on data collection led by a
      BCBA and records two units.
    setting: clinic
