term: Continuing Education Unit
aliases: [CEU, continuing education]
abbreviation: CEU
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    The unit in which behavior analysts' continuing education is counted for
    recertification: a set number per two-year cycle, with minimums in ethics and — for
    those who supervise — in supervision. Units come from learning events by authorized
    providers, university teaching or coursework, and scholarship such as publishing.
    Units cannot be carried into the next cycle and repeated events do not count.
  plain: >-
    The credit analysts earn from continuing education. It is needed to recertify every
    two years. Some must be in ethics. Some must be in supervision if you supervise.
  gloss: Credit for analysts' continuing education
examples:
  - text: >-
      An analyst attends a 50-minute ethics webinar from an authorized provider and
      enters one ethics unit.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Attending the same annual conference talk a second time. Repeated events do not
      count.
