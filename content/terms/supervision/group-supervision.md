term: Group Supervision
aliases: [small-group supervision]
category: supervision
definition:
  technical: >-
    Supervision delivered to more than one supervisee at once, with a cap on group size
    set by the certification requirements. It is efficient for content that is common
    across supervisees — ethics discussion, shared procedures, case presentation — and
    cannot substitute for observation of an individual's own performance.
  plain: >-
    Supervision with several people at once. Good for shared topics, no substitute for
    being watched yourself.
  gloss: Supervision delivered to several supervisees at once
examples:
  - text: >-
      Six supervisees work through an ethics scenario together, each stating what they
      would do and why.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Counting a whole-team staff meeting about rotas as supervision.
    setting: clinic
