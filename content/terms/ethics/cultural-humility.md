id: cultural-humility
term: Cultural Humility
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    An ongoing stance of self-examination about one's own culture, assumptions and
    power, combined with openness to learning about each client's and family's
    identities and values from them rather than assuming. It differs from cultural
    competence in being a lifelong practice rather than a body of knowledge one can
    finish acquiring.
  plain: >-
    Keeping an open mind about other people's cultures and values, learning from them,
    and noticing your own assumptions. It is a habit, not a course you finish.
  gloss: An ongoing, humble stance toward other people's cultures
examples:
  - text: >-
      Before setting mealtime goals, an analyst asks the family how meals work in their
      home and what matters to them, rather than assuming.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Reading one summary about a culture and treating every family from it the same
      way afterward.
    setting: any
