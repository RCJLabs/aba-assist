id: evidence-based-practice
term: Evidence-Based Practice
aliases: [EBP, empirically supported treatment]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    Selecting interventions by combining the best available research evidence with
    the practitioner's expertise and the client's values, preferences and context. All
    three parts count: a procedure with strong research support that the family will
    not use, or that does not fit the setting, is not the evidence-based choice for
    that client.
  plain: >-
    Choosing treatments that research supports, that you know how to deliver, and that
    fit what the client and family want. All three matter.
  gloss: Research evidence, clinical expertise and client values together
examples:
  - text: >-
      Between two well-supported teaching methods, the analyst picks the one the family
      can run at home, and tracks whether it works.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Choosing a method because it is popular online, without checking the research or
      the fit.
    setting: any
