id: conflict-of-interest
term: Conflict of Interest
aliases: [gifts, financial conflict]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    A situation in which a practitioner's personal, financial or other interests could
    compromise, or appear to compromise, their professional judgment on behalf of a
    client or supervisee. Multiple relationships are the most common source; gifts
    above a small value, business arrangements with families, and recommending
    services one profits from are others.
  plain: >-
    When something you stand to gain could sway, or look like it sways, your judgment
    for a client. Gifts and side deals are common examples.
  gloss: A personal interest that could compromise professional judgment
examples:
  - text: >-
      A family offers to pay a technician privately for extra weekend sessions outside
      the agency. The technician declines and tells their supervisor.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Recommending a program because the data show it works for this learner. No
      competing interest is involved.
    setting: any
