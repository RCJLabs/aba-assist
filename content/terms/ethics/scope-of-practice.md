definition:
  technical: >-
    The range of activities a person is qualified and authorized to perform, set by their
    credential, their training, and their supervisor's direction. For technicians it
    centers on implementing plans as written; designing, modifying, discontinuing, and
    interpreting them sit with the supervising analyst, who remains responsible for the
    work.

    What you are trained and allowed to do. Technicians carry out the plan as written.
    Changing the plan or deciding what it means is the supervisor's job.
  gloss: What your role and training allow you to do

Staying in scope protects the learner and it protects you. If you follow an instruction
you were not trained for and it goes wrong, the fact that you were told to do it does not
resolve it.

If you are asked to do something outside your training, say so and document that you said
so. That is the expected response, not an awkward one.
