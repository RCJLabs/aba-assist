definition:
  technical: >-
    The United States federal law whose Privacy and Security Rules govern how covered
    health-care providers and their workforce use, share and protect identifiable
    health information. For most ABA providers billing insurance it applies directly,
    and it sits alongside state privacy law, school privacy law, and the ethics code's
    own confidentiality standards, which apply even where HIPAA does not.
  plain: >-
    The US federal privacy law for health information. It controls who can see or
    share a client's records and what you must do to protect them. Other rules apply
    on top of it.
  gloss: US federal law protecting identifiable health information
