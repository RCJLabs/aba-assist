  technical: >-
    The certifying board's binding standards of professional conduct. Behavior analysts
    and assistant analysts follow the Ethics Code for Behavior Analysts, which is
    organized around four core principles and six sections of standards; technicians
    follow the RBT Ethics Code (2.0), organized in three sections. Both took effect
    on 1 January 2022, both are enforced through a complaint process, and both are
    linked from this app rather than reproduced in it.
