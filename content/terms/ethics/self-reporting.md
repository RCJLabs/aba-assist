term: Self-Reporting
aliases: [self-report requirement, reporting to the board]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    The certificant's duty to notify the certifying board of certain events about
    themselves — investigations, disciplinary actions, some criminal matters, health
    conditions that affect safe practice, and supervision lapses — within the required
    window, which is 30 days for most critical events. It is separate from reporting
    someone else's alleged violation, and the board's own guidance decides what counts.
  plain: >-
    Telling the board yourself when certain things happen to you, like an
    investigation or a lapse in supervision, usually within 30 days. Check the board's
    list; when in doubt, report.
  gloss: The duty to tell the board about certain events in your own conduct
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician who realizes they went a month without required supervision reports
      it to the board and their supervisor.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Filing a complaint about another practitioner's conduct. That is a report about
      someone else, handled through a different process.
    setting: any
