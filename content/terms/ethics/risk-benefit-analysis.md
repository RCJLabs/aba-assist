id: risk-benefit-analysis
term: Risk-Benefit Analysis
aliases: [risk assessment]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    A documented weighing of the harms a proposed intervention may cause against the
    harms of leaving the behaviour unaddressed, including the risks of doing nothing. It
    is required before restrictive procedures and is the record that shows why a less
    restrictive option was judged insufficient rather than simply skipped.
  plain: >-
    Writing down what could go wrong with a plan, what could go wrong without it, and
    why the choice was made.
  gloss: Weighing an intervention's harms against the harm of not acting
examples:
  - text: >-
      Before a restrictive procedure is proposed, the record shows which less
      restrictive options were tried, for how long, and with what result.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Selecting a restrictive procedure first because it is expected to work faster.
    setting: any
