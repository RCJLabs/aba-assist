id: public-statement
term: Public Statement
aliases: [public statements, social media, advertising, testimonials]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    Anything a practitioner communicates to the public about their services, the
    profession, or their clients — advertising, websites, social media posts,
    presentations, media comments. The ethics code requires such statements to be
    truthful and not misleading, and it restricts using client information or images
    without consent and soliciting testimonials from current clients.
  plain: >-
    Anything you say publicly about your work, including on social media. It has to be
    honest, and it must not expose clients or use them for advertising.
  gloss: What a practitioner says publicly about services or clients
examples:
  - text: >-
      An analyst declines to post a video of a session, even a positive one, because
      the child could be identified.
    setting: any
nonExamples:

      A private conversation with a supervisor about a client. That is confidential
      professional communication, not a public statement.
