id: mandated-reporter
term: Mandated Reporter
aliases: [mandatory reporting, duty to report]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    A person required by state law to report reasonable suspicion of abuse or neglect
    of a child or vulnerable adult to the designated authority. In most states that
    includes people who provide services to children, which covers technicians and
    analysts. The standard is reasonable suspicion, not proof; the report goes to
    protective services or law enforcement, not only to an employer; and failing to
    report can itself be an offense.
  plain: >-
    Someone the law requires to report suspected abuse or neglect. You do not need
    proof, only a reasonable suspicion. Report to the authorities, not just your boss.
  gloss: Legally required to report suspected abuse or neglect
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician who notices unexplained injuries and a frightened reaction to a
      caregiver reports to the state hotline the same day and informs their supervisor.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Waiting until there is proof before reporting. The legal standard is suspicion;
      investigating is the agency's job.
    setting: any
