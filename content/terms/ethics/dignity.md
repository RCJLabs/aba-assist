term: Dignity
aliases: [respect, treating clients with dignity]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    The standard that every person receiving services is treated with respect for
    their worth, privacy, choices and autonomy — in how they are spoken to and about,
    in how procedures are delivered, and in what goals are chosen. It is one of the
    core principles of the ethics code, and it is the reason assent, choice and
    avoiding compliance-for-its-own-sake belong in a program rather than outside it.
  plain: >-
    Treating the person as a person: with respect, privacy and real choices. It shapes
    how you talk to them, how you talk about them, and what goals get chosen.
  gloss: Respecting the worth, privacy and choices of the person
