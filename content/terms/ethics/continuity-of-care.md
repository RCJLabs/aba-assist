id: continuity-of-care
term: Continuity of Care
aliases: [transition of services, discontinuing services, service transition]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    The obligation to plan for the person's ongoing services whenever a practitioner
    leaves, a program ends, or a client is transferred — giving notice, documenting the
    plan, handing over to a qualified provider, and not stopping services abruptly.
    Leaving a position without arranging this is a reportable ethics violation.
  plain: >-
    Making sure the client is not left without services when you leave or a program
    ends. Give notice, write it up, and hand over properly.
  gloss: Services continue safely when a provider or program changes
examples:
  - text: >-
      A departing technician gives the agreed notice and reviews the current programs
      with the incoming technician and supervisor.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Quitting by text message the night before a session with no handover.
    setting: any
