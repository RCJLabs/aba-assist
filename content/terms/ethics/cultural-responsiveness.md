term: Cultural Responsiveness
aliases: [culturally responsive practice, contextual fit]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    Adapting assessment, goals, procedures, language and materials so they fit the
    client's and family's culture, values, language and circumstances. Where cultural
    humility is the stance, responsiveness is the action — the concrete changes made to
    services so that they work in this family's life and are acceptable to them.
  plain: >-
    Changing how you deliver services so they fit the family's culture, language and
    daily life. The doing, where humility is the attitude.
  gloss: Adapting services to fit the client's culture and context
