id: scope-of-competence
term: Scope of Competence
aliases: [boundaries of competence, competence]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    The range of activities a particular practitioner can perform well because of their
    training, supervised experience and continuing education — narrower than the scope
    of practice their credential permits. Working outside it requires obtaining
    training or supervision first, or referring elsewhere. It is personal: two people
    with the same credential can have very different scopes of competence.
  plain: >-
    What you personally are trained and experienced enough to do well. It is smaller
    than what your credential allows on paper.
  gloss: What you individually are trained to do well
examples:
  - text: >-
      An analyst experienced with young children in home settings gets supervision
      before taking on an adult with a feeding disorder.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A technician deciding whether to change a program. That is outside the
      credential's scope of practice, regardless of experience.
    setting: clinic
