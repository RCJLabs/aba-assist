id: power-differential
term: Power Differential
aliases: [power imbalance]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    The unequal authority in a supervisory relationship, where one party controls
    sign-off, hours, assignment and reference. It shapes what a supervisee will say,
    decline or report, which makes consent and candor in that relationship things the
    supervisor has to actively create rather than assume from an absence of complaints.
  plain: >-
    The supervisor holds the power in the relationship. That changes what a supervisee
    feels able to say or refuse.
  gloss: Unequal authority in a supervisory relationship
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor states that declining an optional extra shift will not affect
      sign-off, and repeats it when asking.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Treating a supervisee's agreement as freely given because they did not object.
    setting: clinic
