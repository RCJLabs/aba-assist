id: informed-consent
term: Informed Consent
aliases: [consent, consent for services]
category: ethics
definition:
  technical: >-
    Agreement to assessment or treatment given by the client or their legal
    representative after being told, in language they understand, what will be done,
    why, the risks and benefits, the alternatives, and that they may withdraw. It is
    obtained before services begin and again when the plan changes materially, and it
    is distinct from the person's ongoing assent to what is happening in the moment.
  plain: >-
    Permission for services, given by the client or guardian after they understand what
    will happen and can say no. It is different from the person's moment-to-moment
    willingness, which is assent.
  gloss: Permission for services, given with full understanding
examples:
  - text: >-
      Before starting a functional analysis, the analyst explains the conditions and
      risks to the parents and gets written consent.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner pulling away from an activity. That withdrawal is about assent, not the
      legal consent given by a guardian.
    setting: clinic
