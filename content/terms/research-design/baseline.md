id: baseline
term: Baseline
aliases: [A phase, pre-treatment condition]
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    The condition in which the independent variable is absent, measured repeatedly
    until responding is stable enough to predict what would happen if nothing changed.
    Baseline data are the comparison against which every treatment effect is judged, and
    a baseline that is trending in the direction of the intended change cannot support a
    clear conclusion.
  plain: >-
    Measuring the behavior before treatment starts, long enough to see its usual
    pattern. Everything later is compared to this.
  gloss: Measurement before the treatment begins
examples:
  - text: >-
      Five sessions of counting requests before any teaching begins, so the team can see
      whether teaching changes the count.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      One quick observation on the first day. A single point cannot show a pattern or
      predict what would happen without treatment.
    setting: clinic
