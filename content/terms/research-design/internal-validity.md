id: internal-validity
term: Internal Validity
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    The degree to which a demonstration rules out explanations other than the
    independent variable for the change observed. High internal validity means the
    design controlled for confounds well enough that the treatment is the most
    plausible cause.
  plain: >-
    How sure you can be that the treatment, and not something else, caused the change.
  gloss: Confidence the treatment caused the change
examples:
  - text: >-
      A multiple-baseline shows improvement in each setting only when treatment reaches
      it. Maturation or a change at home cannot explain that pattern.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      An A-B comparison where a new medication started the same week as the plan.
      Either could explain the change, so internal validity is low.
    setting: any
contrastWith: [external-validity]
