id: changing-criterion-design
term: Changing Criterion Design
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    A single-case design in which the performance required for reinforcement is raised
    or lowered in a series of steps, and control is shown when behavior tracks each new
    criterion. It suits behavior that is already occurring and needs to change
    gradually in amount, such as increasing steps walked or decreasing cigarettes smoked.
  plain: >-
    Raise the bar a little at a time and see whether the behavior follows each step.
    Good for behavior that needs to change gradually.
  gloss: Behavior tracked against a stepwise criterion
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner earns a break after 3 problems, then 5, then 8, and their work output
      rises to match each step.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Teaching a brand-new skill from zero. The design needs a behavior that already
      happens and can be scaled.
    setting: any
