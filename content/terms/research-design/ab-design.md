id: ab-design
term: A-B Design
aliases: [AB design, pre-post comparison]
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    A two-phase arrangement — baseline followed by treatment — with no withdrawal or
    staggering. It shows whether behavior changed after treatment began, but because
    nothing rules out other explanations it does not demonstrate a functional relation.
    It is common in practice and weak as evidence.
  plain: >-
    Measure before, then measure during treatment. It shows a change but cannot prove
    the treatment caused it.
  gloss: Baseline then treatment, with no replication
examples:
  - text: >-
      Two weeks of baseline data on tantrums, then the plan starts and tantrums fall.
      Encouraging, but not yet proof.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The same plan withdrawn and reintroduced with tantrums rising and falling in
      step. The added phases make it a reversal design.
    setting: home
