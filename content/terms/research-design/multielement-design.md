term: Multielement Design
aliases: [alternating treatments design, ATD, multiple-treatment design]
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    A single-case design in which two or more conditions are alternated rapidly, often
    session by session in a random or counterbalanced order, and their effects are
    compared by the separation between the data paths. It is the design behind most
    functional analyses and a common choice for comparing two teaching procedures.
  plain: >-
    Switch quickly between two or more conditions and see which one produces more of
    the behavior. Used to compare treatments and in functional analysis.
  gloss: Conditions alternated rapidly and compared
examples:
  - text: >-
      Attention, demand, alone and play conditions are rotated across sessions, and the
      behavior is far higher in the attention condition.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Running one teaching method for a month and then a different one for a month.
      That is a sequential comparison without rapid alternation.
    setting: clinic
