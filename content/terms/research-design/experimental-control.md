id: experimental-control
term: Experimental Control
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    The state of affairs in which the experimenter can make the behavior change by
    manipulating the independent variable, with confounds ruled out. In single-case
    designs it is shown by prediction, verification and replication: baseline predicts
    what would happen without treatment, a return to baseline verifies the prediction,
    and reintroducing treatment replicates the effect.
  plain: >-
    Being able to turn the behavior change on and off by changing the treatment. That is
    what proves the treatment is responsible.
  gloss: Showing the treatment reliably produces the change
examples:
  - text: >-
      Each time a token system is in place the behavior improves, and each time it is
      withdrawn the behavior returns. The team has experimental control.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Behavior improves after treatment starts and never returns when treatment is
      paused. The effect may be real, but control has not been shown.
    setting: any
