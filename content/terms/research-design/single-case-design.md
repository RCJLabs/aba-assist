id: single-case-design
term: Single-Case Experimental Design
aliases: [single-subject design, SCED, within-subject design, N-of-1 design]
abbreviation: SCED
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    A family of experimental designs in which each participant serves as their own
    control. Behavior is measured repeatedly, conditions are introduced and withdrawn or
    staggered according to a plan, and control is demonstrated by predicting,
    verifying and replicating effects within that one person's data rather than by
    comparing group averages.
  plain: >-
    Experiments done with one person at a time. The person is compared to themselves
    under different conditions, with lots of repeated measurement.
  gloss: Experiments where each person is their own control
examples:
  - text: >-
      Baseline for two weeks, treatment for two weeks, back to baseline, then treatment
      again, all with one learner and daily data. That is an A-B-A-B single-case design.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Comparing the average score of a treated class with an untreated class. That is a
      group design.
    setting: school
