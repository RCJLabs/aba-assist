id: external-validity
term: External Validity
aliases: [generality of findings]
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    The degree to which a finding holds beyond the people, settings, behaviors and
    conditions in which it was demonstrated. In behavior analysis it is established by
    systematic replication rather than by sampling from a population.
  plain: >-
    Whether a result would hold with other people, in other places, or for other
    behaviors.
  gloss: Whether a finding extends beyond the original case
examples:
  - text: >-
      A prompting method shown to work with three learners across two clinics has more
      external validity than one shown with a single learner.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Showing, with a reversal, that a plan worked for one learner. That establishes
      internal validity for that learner, not that it works for others.
    setting: clinic
