---
id: independent-variable
term: Independent Variable
aliases: [IV, treatment variable, intervention]
abbreviation: IV
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    The condition that the experimenter or clinician manipulates on purpose to see
    whether it changes the dependent variable. In applied work it is the intervention
    itself, and procedural integrity is the measure of whether it was actually delivered.
  plain: >-
    The thing you change on purpose, usually the treatment, to see what happens to the
    behavior.
  gloss: The treatment or condition being changed on purpose
examples:
  - text: >-
      Adding praise for every hand raise is the independent variable in a study of
      hand raising.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The number of hand raises. That is what is measured, so it is the dependent
      variable.
    setting: school
