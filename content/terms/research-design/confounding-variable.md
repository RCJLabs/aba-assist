id: confounding-variable
term: Confounding Variable
aliases: [confound, extraneous variable, threat to internal validity]
category: research-design
definition:
  technical: >-
    An uncontrolled variable that changes along with the independent variable and could
    account for the observed effect. Common confounds in applied work include events
    outside the program, maturation, changes in measurement, and the intervention being
    delivered differently over time.
  plain: >-
    Something else that changed at the same time as the treatment and might be the real
    reason the behavior changed.
  gloss: Another possible cause that was not controlled
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student's disruptions fall the week the new plan starts, which is also the week
      a disruptive classmate moves away.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A variable the design holds constant across phases, such as the same observer
      recording in every condition.
    setting: any
