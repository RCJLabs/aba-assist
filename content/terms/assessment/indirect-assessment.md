id: indirect-assessment
term: Indirect Assessment
aliases: [informant assessment, interview, rating scale, questionnaire]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    Gathering information about a behavior from people who know the person — through
    interviews, checklists, rating scales and record reviews — rather than by observing
    the behavior directly. It is quick and useful for planning, and its results are
    reports about behavior rather than measures of it, so they are treated as a starting
    point.
  plain: >-
    Asking people who know the person about the behavior, using interviews or forms.
    Quick and helpful, but it is what people remember, not what was observed.
  gloss: Information about behavior gathered from informants
examples:
  - text: >-
      A structured interview with a parent about when the behavior is most and least
      likely and what usually follows it.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Sitting in the classroom and recording each occurrence. That is direct observation.
    setting: school
