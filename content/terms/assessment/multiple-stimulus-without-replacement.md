term: Multiple-Stimulus Without Replacement
aliases: [MSWO, multiple stimulus without replacement preference assessment]
abbreviation: MSWO
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A preference assessment in which an array of items is presented, the person selects
    one, the selected item is removed, and the remaining items are re-presented until
    all have been chosen or the person stops choosing. The order of selection gives the
    ranking. It is fast and is widely used to pick reinforcers before a session.
  plain: >-
    Lay out several items, let the person pick one, take that one away, and repeat with
    what is left. The order they pick gives the ranking. Quick to run.
  gloss: An array of items; each choice removed, then repeated
examples:
  - text: >-
      Five snacks are laid out; the learner picks the crackers, which are removed, then
      the raisins from the remaining four, and so on.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The chosen item is put back and the array is presented again. That is
      multiple-stimulus with replacement.
    setting: clinic
