id: free-operant-preference-assessment
term: Free-Operant Preference Assessment
aliases: [free operant observation, naturalistic preference assessment]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A preference assessment in which the person has free access to several items or
    activities at once and the observer records how long they engage with each. Nothing
    is removed and no choice is forced. It is quick, low-effort for the person, and
    useful when removing items would itself provoke problem behavior.
  plain: >-
    Let the person have everything at once and time how long they spend with each thing.
    Nothing is taken away, so it is gentle.
  gloss: Free access to items; engagement time recorded
examples:
  - text: >-
      Over five minutes in a play area, the learner spends three minutes at the sand
      table and none with the puzzles.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Presenting items in pairs and recording the choice. That is a paired-stimulus
      format.
    setting: clinic
