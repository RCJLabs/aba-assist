term: Functional Behavior Assessment
aliases: [FBA, functional assessment, functional analysis, FA]
abbreviation: FBA
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A set of procedures for identifying the conditions under which a behavior occurs and
    the consequences maintaining it, combining interview and record review, direct
    observation, and in some cases an experimental analysis in which conditions are
    arranged and compared. Its output is a statement of function that drives treatment.
  plain: >-
    Finding out why a behavior keeps happening and what it gets for the person. It uses
    interviews, watching, and sometimes a careful test of different conditions.
  gloss: Finding out what keeps a behavior going
examples:
  - text: >-
      Staff record what happened before and after each occurrence across two weeks, and an
      analyst reviews the pattern.
    setting: school
  - text: >-
      An analyst arranges brief, controlled conditions to compare how behavior differs when
      attention, escape, or access is available.
    setting: clinic
    why: Comparing arranged conditions is what separates an analysis from a description.
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Deciding a behavior is attention seeking because it looks that way, without data.
      That is a guess, not an assessment.
    setting: any
  - text: >-
      Finding out which snacks a learner prefers. That is a preference assessment and
      answers a different question.
    setting: clinic

Designing, conducting and interpreting a functional assessment is the supervising
analyst's work. A technician's part is usually accurate recording — which is the part the
whole conclusion rests on.
