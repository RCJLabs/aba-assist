term: Skills Assessment
aliases: [skill assessment, criterion-referenced assessment, curriculum-based assessment]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    An evaluation of what a person can currently do across areas such as communication,
    social, self-care, play and academic skills, usually against a fixed set of
    criteria rather than against other people. Direct testing and observation are
    preferred over informant report. The results identify skills to teach and the order
    to teach them in.
  plain: >-
    Finding out which skills a person already has and which they still need. It is
    used to choose what to teach next.
  gloss: Assessing what a person can currently do
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner is asked to complete a series of matching, imitation and requesting
      tasks, and the first missing skills become teaching targets.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Assessing why a learner hits. That is a functional assessment, about problem
      behavior rather than skills.
    setting: clinic
