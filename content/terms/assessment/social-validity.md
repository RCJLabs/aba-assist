id: social-validity
term: Social Validity
aliases: [social significance, consumer satisfaction]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    The judgment, by the people affected, of whether the goals of an intervention are
    important, its procedures are acceptable, and its outcomes are meaningful. It is
    assessed by asking clients, caregivers and others, and it matters because a
    technically successful program that nobody values or will keep using has not
    succeeded.
  plain: >-
    Whether the people involved think the goal matters, the method is acceptable, and the
    result made a real difference. Their opinion counts.
  gloss: Whether the people affected find the goals, methods and results worthwhile
examples:
  - text: >-
      After a program, parents report that mealtimes are calmer and that they are happy
      to keep using the procedure.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A behavior reduced by 80 percent using a procedure the family found humiliating
      and stopped using. The data are good; the social validity is poor.
    setting: home
