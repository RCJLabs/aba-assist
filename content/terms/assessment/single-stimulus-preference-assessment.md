id: single-stimulus-preference-assessment
term: Single-Stimulus Preference Assessment
aliases: [successive choice, SS preference assessment]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A preference assessment in which items are presented one at a time and the person's
    response to each — approach, engagement, or duration of contact — is recorded. It is
    the simplest format and suits people who do not yet choose between two items, but
    it can overrate items because nothing is being compared.
  plain: >-
    Show one item at a time and see how the person reacts to it. Simple, and good for
    people who cannot yet pick between two things.
  gloss: Items presented one at a time; reaction recorded
examples:
  - text: >-
      A bubble wand is offered on its own; the learner reaches for it and plays for
      two minutes. Then a ball is offered on its own.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Two items offered together and the chosen one recorded. That is a paired-stimulus
      format.
    setting: clinic
