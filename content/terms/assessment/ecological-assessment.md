id: ecological-assessment
term: Ecological Assessment
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    An assessment that examines the person's whole environment — physical settings,
    schedules, people, expectations and available reinforcers — across the places they
    spend time, rather than a single behavior in a single setting. It tends to reveal
    mismatches between demands and skills that a narrower assessment would miss.
  plain: >-
    Looking at the person's whole day and all their settings, not just one behavior in
    one place. It finds mismatches between what is expected and what the person can do.
  gloss: Assessing the person's full environment across settings
examples:
  - text: >-
      An analyst visits home, school and the after-school program to see how routines,
      demands and available activities differ.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A functional analysis of one behavior in a clinic room. That is narrow by design.
    setting: clinic
