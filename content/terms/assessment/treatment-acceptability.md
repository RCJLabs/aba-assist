id: treatment-acceptability
term: Treatment Acceptability
aliases: [intervention acceptability]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    The extent to which the people affected by an intervention — the client, the family,
    the staff delivering it — judge it appropriate, fair and reasonable for the problem.
    It is one component of social validity, and it is measured before implementation and
    monitored during it, because an unacceptable procedure is not usually refused
    outright but quietly not used.
  plain: >-
    Whether the people involved think a procedure is reasonable. One they dislike often
    gets dropped rather than argued about.
  gloss: Whether those affected judge a procedure reasonable and fair
examples:
  - text: >-
      A family is asked how they feel about a planned procedure before it starts, and a
      component they object to is replaced.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asking about acceptability in the final report, once the programme has already
      finished.
    setting: any
