term: ABC Recording
aliases: [ABC data, antecedent-behavior-consequence recording, ABC chart]
abbreviation: ABC
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A descriptive assessment method in which the observer records, for each occurrence
    of the target behavior, what happened immediately before it and what happened
    immediately after it, in the natural setting. Over many occurrences it reveals
    patterns that suggest a function; it does not by itself prove one, because nothing
    is manipulated.
  plain: >-
    Writing down what happened right before a behavior, the behavior itself, and what
    happened right after. Patterns across many entries hint at why it happens.
  gloss: Recording what came before and after each behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      An entry reads: told to clean up; threw blocks; activity ended and staff sat with
      him. Repeated entries like this point toward escape or attention.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A questionnaire filled in by a parent about when the behavior usually happens. That
      is an indirect assessment.
    setting: home
