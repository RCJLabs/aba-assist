term: Function of Behavior
aliases: [four functions, functions of behavior, attention, escape, tangible, automatic, SEAT]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    The purpose a behavior serves for the person, defined by the consequence that
    maintains it. Functional assessment usually sorts maintaining consequences into
    four kinds: attention from others, escape or avoidance of demands and situations,
    access to items or activities, and automatic (sensory) consequences produced by the
    behavior itself. Treatment is matched to function, not to what the behavior looks
    like.
  plain: >-
    Why a behavior keeps happening — what it gets the person. Usually one of four
    things: attention, getting out of something, getting an item, or a feeling the
    behavior itself makes.
  gloss: The consequence that keeps a behavior going
