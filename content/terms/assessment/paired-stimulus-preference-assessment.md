id: paired-stimulus-preference-assessment
term: Paired-Stimulus Preference Assessment
aliases: [paired choice, forced choice, PS preference assessment]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A preference assessment in which items are presented two at a time, every item is
    paired with every other item, and the item chosen in each pair is recorded. Items
    are then ranked by how often they were chosen. It produces a clear hierarchy and
    takes longer than the multiple-stimulus formats.
  plain: >-
    Show two items at a time and record which one the person picks. Every item gets
    paired with every other one, which gives a clear ranking.
  gloss: Items presented in pairs; choices ranked
examples:
  - text: >-
      Six items produce fifteen pairs. The tablet is chosen in all five of its pairs
      and ranks first.
    setting: clinic
  - text: >-
      Items are shown two at a time until every item has been offered against every other, and
      the number of times each was picked becomes its rank.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      All six items laid out at once and the chosen one removed each time. That is
      multiple-stimulus without replacement.
    setting: clinic
contrastWith: [multiple-stimulus-without-replacement]
