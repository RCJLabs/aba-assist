id: scatterplot
term: Scatterplot
aliases: [scatter plot, scatterplot analysis]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    A descriptive-assessment grid with time of day on one axis and successive days on
    the other, marked to show when the behavior occurred and how much. Over days it
    reveals whether the behavior clusters at particular times, activities or
    transitions, which narrows where to look for the maintaining variables.
  plain: >-
    A grid of days and times, marked whenever the behavior happens. After a while it
    shows the times of day when the behavior is worst.
  gloss: A grid showing when in the day behavior occurs
examples:
  - text: >-
      Two weeks of marks show almost all incidents fall between 11:00 and 11:30, just
      before lunch.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A line graph of daily totals. It shows how much per day, not when in the day.
    setting: school
