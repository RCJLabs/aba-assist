term: Functional Analysis
aliases: [FA, experimental functional analysis, analog assessment]
abbreviation: FA
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    An experimental method for identifying the function of a behavior: conditions that
    each present one possible antecedent and consequence — attention, escape from
    demands, access to tangibles, alone — are arranged and alternated, and the behavior
    is measured in each. The condition with the highest rate identifies the maintaining
    consequence. It is the only assessment method that demonstrates function rather
    than suggesting it, and it is designed and interpreted by the supervising analyst.
  plain: >-
    A test of why a behavior happens. Different situations are set up on purpose, and
    the one where the behavior happens most shows what keeps it going. The analyst
    designs and reads it.
  gloss: An experiment that identifies why a behavior occurs
examples:
  - text: >-
      Across alternating sessions, hair pulling is high only when it produces a break
      from tasks. Escape is the demonstrated function.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:

      Recording ABC data during a normal morning. Nothing is arranged, so it is a
      descriptive assessment.
    setting: school
