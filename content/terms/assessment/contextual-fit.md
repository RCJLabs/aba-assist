id: contextual-fit
term: Contextual Fit
aliases: [goodness of fit]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    The degree to which an intervention matches the values, skills, resources and
    routines of the people who will deliver it and the setting it will run in.
    Contextual fit is assessed before a procedure is chosen rather than discovered
    afterwards, because a procedure the setting cannot sustain will be delivered poorly
    or abandoned, and the resulting flat data will be read as evidence that the
    procedure does not work.
  plain: >-
    Whether a plan actually suits the people who have to run it and the place it runs
    in. A good plan nobody can deliver is not a good plan.
  gloss: Whether a plan suits the people and setting that must run it
examples:
  - text: >-
      A plan requiring five minutes of one-to-one attention every hour is rewritten for
      a classroom with one adult and eight children.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Writing a technically strong plan and asking the team to find a way to make it
      work.
    setting: any
