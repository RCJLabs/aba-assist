id: descriptive-assessment
term: Descriptive Assessment
aliases: [descriptive analysis, direct observation assessment]
category: assessment
definition:
  technical: >-
    Direct observation of the behavior and the events around it under naturally
    occurring conditions, without changing anything. ABC recording, scatter plots and
    structured observation forms are the common tools. It yields correlations between
    events and behavior that can generate hypotheses, which a functional analysis can
    then test.
  plain: >-
    Watching the behavior where it normally happens and recording what goes on around
    it. It shows patterns but does not test them.
  gloss: Observing behavior in its natural setting without changing anything
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician observes a whole morning and notes each outburst along with the
      activity, the people present and what followed.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Arranging an attention condition and a demand condition and comparing them. That
      manipulation makes it a functional analysis.
    setting: clinic
contrastWith: [functional-analysis, indirect-assessment]
