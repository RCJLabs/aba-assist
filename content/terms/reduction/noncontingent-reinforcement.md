term: Noncontingent Reinforcement
aliases:
  [NCR, time-based reinforcement, fixed-time schedule, response-independent reinforcement]
abbreviation: NCR
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    Delivering the reinforcer that maintains a problem behavior on a time-based
    schedule, independent of the behavior — attention every two minutes regardless of
    what the person is doing. It works as an abolishing operation, reducing the
    behavior because its reinforcer is freely available, and it is often combined with
    extinction. Accidental pairing with the problem behavior is the main risk.
  plain: >-
    Giving the person the thing the behavior used to get — like attention — for free,
    on a timer. The behavior drops because it is no longer needed.
  gloss: The maintaining reinforcer delivered on a timer, not for behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner whose shouting produced attention now gets a staff member's attention
      every ninety seconds whether or not they shout.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Attention delivered only after thirty seconds without shouting. Because it
      depends on the absence of behavior, that is DRO.
    setting: clinic
