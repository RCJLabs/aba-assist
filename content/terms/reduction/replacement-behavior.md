id: replacement-behavior
term: Replacement Behavior
aliases: [alternative behavior, functionally equivalent replacement behavior]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A behavior chosen to take the place of a problem behavior because it serves the
    same function, is acceptable to others, and is at least as easy to perform and as
    reliably reinforced. A reduction plan without a replacement leaves the person with
    a need and no way to meet it, which is where relapse comes from.
  plain: >-
    A better behavior that gets the person the same thing the problem behavior did. A
    plan should always include one.
  gloss: An acceptable behavior that serves the same purpose
examples:
  - text: >-
      Tapping a peer's shoulder replaces pulling their hair as a way to start play.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Sitting quietly, offered as the replacement for hair pulling that was about
      starting play. It does not get the person what they were after.
    setting: school
