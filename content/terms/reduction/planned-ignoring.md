---
id: planned-ignoring
term: Planned Ignoring
aliases: [attention extinction]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    Withholding attention — eye contact, comment, physical contact — following a
    behavior that is maintained by attention, as written into a plan. It is extinction
    for attention-maintained behavior, not a general response to misbehavior: ignoring
    a behavior maintained by escape or by automatic reinforcement does nothing, and
    ignoring a dangerous behavior is never the plan.
  plain: >-
    Not giving attention after a behavior that happens to get attention. It only makes
    sense when attention was the reward. It is never used for anything unsafe.
  gloss: Withholding attention from attention-maintained behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner's silly noises used to get laughs. Staff now carry on without reacting,
      as the plan directs, and praise the learner when they speak normally.
    setting: school
  - text: >-
      A child who used to get a reaction for repeating a rude word says it at dinner and the
      conversation carries on around them, exactly as the plan directs.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Ignoring a learner's protests about a task. If the protest was about escaping the
      task, attention was never the reinforcer and ignoring it changes nothing.
    setting: school
