---
id: escape-extinction
term: Escape Extinction
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    Extinction applied to behavior maintained by negative reinforcement: the behavior no
    longer ends or postpones the demand or situation it used to escape. The demand
    stays in place and is followed through. It is designed by the supervising analyst,
    written into the plan with what follow-through means for that learner, and it is
    not something a technician improvises.
  plain: >-
    When a behavior used to get the person out of something, it stops working: the task
    stays. The plan says exactly how. Never improvised.
  gloss: The behavior no longer ends the demand
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who used to end a puzzle task by pushing it away now finds the puzzle
      stays until the plan's step is complete.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Ignoring a behavior that used to get attention. That is extinction of an
      attention-maintained behavior, not escape extinction.
    setting: clinic
