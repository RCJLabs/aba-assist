id: reinstatement
term: Reinstatement
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    The return of a previously extinguished behavior after the reinforcer that
    maintained it is encountered again, independently of the behavior. A single
    unearned contact with the old consequence can be enough to bring responding back,
    which is why accidental delivery during maintenance is worth planning against rather
    than treating as a one-off.
  plain: >-
    The old behavior comes back because the old reward showed up again by chance. The
    person did nothing to earn it.
  gloss: Extinguished behavior returning after the old reinforcer reappears
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner whose screaming no longer produces a break happens to get a break when a
      fire alarm interrupts the lesson, and screaming returns that afternoon.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A behavior returning after several weeks without it and without any contact with
      the old consequence, which is spontaneous recovery.
    setting: any
