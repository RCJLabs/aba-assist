id: differential-reinforcement-of-incompatible-behavior
term: Differential Reinforcement of Incompatible Behavior
aliases: [DRI]
abbreviation: DRI
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A differential-reinforcement procedure in which reinforcement is delivered for a
    behavior that cannot physically occur at the same time as the problem behavior,
    and withheld for the problem behavior. It is a special case of DRA in which the
    alternative is chosen for its incompatibility — hands in pockets cannot also be
    hands on a peer.
  plain: >-
    Rewarding a behavior the person cannot do at the same time as the problem
    behavior. Hands holding a toy cannot also be hitting.
  gloss: Reinforce a behavior that cannot happen alongside the problem behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who grabs food from others' plates is reinforced for keeping both hands
      on their own tray during meals.
    setting: home
  - text: >-
      A learner who mouths materials is reinforced every two minutes for holding a chewy
      necklace between their teeth instead.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Reinforcing asking for a snack instead of grabbing. Asking is an alternative, but
      a person can ask and grab at once, so that is DRA rather than DRI.
    setting: home
