id: differential-reinforcement-of-high-rates
term: Differential Reinforcement of High Rates
aliases: [DRH, DRH schedule]
abbreviation: DRH
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A schedule in which reinforcement is delivered only when the behavior occurs at or
    above a set rate — a minimum number of responses within a period, or responses
    separated by no more than a maximum time. It is used to increase the pace of a
    behavior that already occurs but too slowly, such as work completion.
  plain: >-
    Rewarding the person only when they do the behavior fast enough or often enough.
    Used to speed up a behavior that already happens.
  gloss: Reinforce only when the behavior occurs at a high enough rate
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner earns a break only if they complete at least eight problems in five
      minutes.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Earning a break for completing three or fewer problems' worth of requests for
      help. Reinforcing a lower rate is DRL.
    setting: school
