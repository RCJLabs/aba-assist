id: overcorrection
term: Overcorrection
aliases: [restitutional overcorrection, positive-practice overcorrection]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A positive-punishment procedure that requires effortful behavior following the
    problem behavior. Restitution requires repairing the environment to a state better
    than before; positive practice requires repeating a correct form of the behavior
    many times. Both are restrictive, require close supervision and written
    authorization, and are considered only when less restrictive procedures have failed.
  plain: >-
    After a problem behavior, the person has to do a lot of effortful work, either
    fixing things or practicing the right behavior many times. A restrictive procedure.
  gloss: Effortful repair or practice required after a behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      After tipping a chair, a learner is required to straighten every chair in the
      room.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asking a learner to pick up the one chair they tipped. Simple correction restores
      things as they were and is not overcorrection.
    setting: school
