id: time-out
term: Time-Out From Positive Reinforcement
aliases: [time-out, exclusionary time-out, nonexclusionary time-out]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A negative-punishment procedure in which access to reinforcement is removed for a
    set period following a behavior. Nonexclusionary forms leave the person in the
    setting without access; exclusionary forms move them out of it. It only works when
    the setting was reinforcing to begin with, it is ineffective for escape-maintained
    behavior, and its use is governed by the written plan, employer policy and law.
  plain: >-
    Taking away the chance to earn rewards for a short time after a behavior. It only
    works if the person wanted to be where they were.
  gloss: Removing access to reinforcement for a period
examples:
  - text: >-
      A child who throws game pieces sits out of the game for one minute, then rejoins.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Sending a student out of a lesson they were trying to escape. Removal there is a
      reward, not a punishment.
    setting: school
