term: Least Restrictive Alternative
aliases:
  [least restrictive procedure, least intrusive intervention, hierarchy of restrictiveness]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    The principle that the intervention chosen should be the least intrusive one that
    is likely to be effective, with more restrictive procedures — punishment,
    restrictive consequences, anything involving physical contact — considered only
    after reinforcement-based options have been tried or ruled out, and used only with
    additional safeguards and oversight. It is written into the ethics code and into
    most state regulations.
  plain: >-
    Always try the gentlest thing that is likely to work first. Stronger, more
    restrictive procedures come last and need extra approval and oversight.
  gloss: Choose the gentlest intervention likely to work
examples:
  - text: >-
      Before considering any punishment procedure, a team tries antecedent changes,
      functional communication training and differential reinforcement.
    setting: any
