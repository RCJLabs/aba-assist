id: response-cost
term: Response Cost
aliases: [fine, token loss]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A negative-punishment procedure in which a set amount of a reinforcer already
    possessed — tokens, points, minutes of an activity — is removed following a
    behavior. It is usually built into a token economy. Removing more than the person
    has, or removing so much that earning stops being worthwhile, defeats it.
  plain: >-
    Losing something already earned, like a token or points, after a behavior. A fine.
  gloss: A reinforcer already earned is taken away after a behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student loses one of their five tokens each time they leave their seat without
      permission.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A student sitting apart from the group for two minutes. Losing the chance to earn,
      rather than losing what was earned, is time-out.
    setting: school
