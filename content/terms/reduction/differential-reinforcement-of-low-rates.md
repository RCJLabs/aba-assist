id: differential-reinforcement-of-low-rates
term: Differential Reinforcement of Low Rates
aliases: [DRL, DRL schedule]
abbreviation: DRL
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A procedure for reducing, without eliminating, a behavior that is acceptable at a
    lower rate: reinforcement is delivered when the behavior occurs at or below a set
    number in a period, or when a minimum time has passed since the last response. It
    suits behavior such as asking questions or requesting the bathroom that should
    continue, just less often.
  plain: >-
    Rewarding the person for doing a behavior less often, not for stopping it. Used for
    things that are fine in small amounts.
  gloss: Reinforce the behavior only when it occurs at a lower rate
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student who asks to sharpen a pencil twenty times a day earns a token for each
      hour with three or fewer requests.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Reinforcing the student for going a whole hour with no requests at all. The
      target there is zero, which makes it DRO.
    setting: school
