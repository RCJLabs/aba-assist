id: sensory-extinction
term: Sensory Extinction
aliases: [extinction of automatically reinforced behavior]
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    Extinction applied to behavior maintained by automatic reinforcement: the sensory
    consequence the behavior produces is masked or removed so that the behavior no
    longer produces it — padding a surface, changing a material. Because the
    reinforcer is not delivered by a person, this is the only way to withhold it, and
    it is often hard to do completely.
  plain: >-
    When a behavior is kept going by how it feels or sounds, the plan changes things so
    it no longer produces that feeling or sound.
  gloss: The behavior no longer produces its sensory result
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who flicks a light switch for the clicking sound finds the switch has
      been replaced with a silent one.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A task that no longer goes away when the learner protests. That is escape
      extinction.
    setting: clinic
