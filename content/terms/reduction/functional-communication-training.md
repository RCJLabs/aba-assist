term: Functional Communication Training
aliases: [FCT]
abbreviation: FCT
category: reduction
definition:
  technical: >-
    A treatment in which the person is taught a communicative response — a word, sign,
    card or device press — that produces the same reinforcer as the problem behavior,
    while the problem behavior stops producing it. It is a form of differential
    reinforcement of alternative behavior matched to function, and it is among the
    best-supported treatments for problem behavior.
  plain: >-
    Teaching the person a way to ask for what the problem behavior used to get. The
    asking works; the problem behavior stops working.
  gloss: Teach a request that replaces the problem behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who screamed to end tasks is taught to hand over a break card, and
      break cards are honored while screaming no longer ends the task.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Teaching the learner to say "please" for a snack when the screaming was about
      escaping work. The new response does not serve the same function.
    setting: school
