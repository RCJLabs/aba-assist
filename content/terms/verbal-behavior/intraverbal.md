id: intraverbal
term: Intraverbal
aliases: [conversation, answering questions, fill-in]
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    A verbal operant evoked by someone else's verbal behavior where the response does
    not match it in form — answering a question, finishing a phrase, continuing a
    conversation. Reinforced socially. Most conversation and most academic responding
    is intraverbal.
  plain: >-
    Answering a question or keeping a conversation going. Your words are set off by
    someone else's words, but you do not repeat them.
  gloss: Responding to words with different words
examples:
  - text: >-
      Asked "What do you eat with?", the learner says "a spoon."
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asked to say "spoon," the learner says "spoon." That matches the model, so it is
      an echoic.
    setting: clinic
