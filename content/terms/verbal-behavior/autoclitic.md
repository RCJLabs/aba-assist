id: autoclitic
term: Autoclitic
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    Verbal behavior about the speaker's own verbal behavior, which modifies its effect
    on the listener — "I think" before a claim, "not" inside a sentence, word order and
    grammatical tags. Autoclitics ride on other verbal operants and tell the listener how
    strong, how certain or how to interpret what is being said.
  plain: >-
    The parts of speech that comment on your own words, like "I think" or "maybe."
    They tell the listener how sure you are or how to take what you said.
  gloss: Speech that modifies the effect of the speaker's other speech
examples:
  - text: >-
      "It might be raining" versus "It is raining." The word might changes what the
      listener does with the statement.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Saying "raining" while looking out at rain. That is a plain tact with no
      modifying frame.
    setting: any
