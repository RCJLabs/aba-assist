id: transcription
term: Transcription
aliases: [taking dictation, writing from speech]
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    A verbal operant in which a spoken stimulus evokes a written response that
    corresponds to it — writing or typing what is heard. Copying written text from
    written text is a related operant in which the stimulus and response share the
    same form.
  plain: >-
    Writing down what you hear. Someone says a word and you write or type it.
  gloss: Writing what was heard
examples:
  - text: >-
      The teacher says "cat" and the student writes c-a-t.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The student sees the word "cat" and reads it aloud. Print to speech is textual.
    setting: school
