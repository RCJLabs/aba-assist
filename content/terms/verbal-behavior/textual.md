---
id: textual
term: Textual
aliases: [reading aloud, textual behavior]
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    A verbal operant in which a written or printed stimulus evokes a spoken response
    that corresponds to it — reading aloud — without any requirement that the reader
    understands it. Comprehension involves other operants layered on top.
  plain: >-
    Reading words out loud. Saying the word the print shows, whether or not you know
    what it means.
  gloss: Saying what a written text shows
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner sees the word "stop" on a sign and says "stop."
    setting: community
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner hears "stop" and writes it. Hearing to writing is transcription.
    setting: school
