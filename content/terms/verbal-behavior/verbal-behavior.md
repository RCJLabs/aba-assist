term: Verbal Behavior
aliases: [VB, Skinner's analysis of language]
abbreviation: VB
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    Skinner's analysis of language as behavior: responses that are reinforced through
    the action of another person who has been trained to respond to them. It classifies
    utterances by their controlling variables — what evokes them and what consequence
    maintains them — rather than by their form, so the same word can be a different
    verbal operant on different occasions. Speech, sign, pictures and typing all count.
  plain: >-
    A way of looking at language as behavior. Words are sorted by what causes them and
    what they get, not by what they sound like. Signs and pictures count too.
  gloss: Language analyzed as behavior by its causes and effects
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner says "juice" once to get juice and once to name a picture. Same word,
      two different verbal operants.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Sorting a learner's words into nouns and verbs. That classifies by form, which is
      not the verbal-behavior approach.
    setting: any
