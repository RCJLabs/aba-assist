id: listener-responding
term: Listener Responding
aliases: [receptive language, receptive identification, LR, LRFFC]
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    Nonverbal behavior under the control of someone else's verbal behavior — following
    an instruction, touching the named picture, handing over the requested item. It is
    the listener's half of a verbal episode. Listener responding by feature, function
    and class extends it to items identified by description rather than by name.
  plain: >-
    Doing what words tell you, like pointing to the picture someone names or handing
    over the item they asked for. It is understanding shown by action.
  gloss: Acting on someone else's words
examples:
  - text: >-
      Told "touch the one you eat with," the learner touches the picture of a fork.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The learner says "fork" when shown a fork. Naming is a tact, a speaker skill.
    setting: clinic
