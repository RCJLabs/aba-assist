id: echoic
term: Echoic
aliases: [vocal imitation, echoing]
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    A verbal operant in which the response repeats a verbal stimulus with the same form
    — the speaker says what was just said. It is controlled by the sound of the model
    and reinforced socially, and it is a key building block because a learner who can
    echo can be prompted into new mands and tacts.
  plain: >-
    Repeating what someone just said. It matters because a person who can repeat a word
    can be taught to use it.
  gloss: Repeating what was just said
examples:
  - text: >-
      The technician says "cookie" and the learner says "cookie."
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The technician says "What do you want?" and the learner says "cookie." The
      answer does not match the question, so it is an intraverbal or a mand.
    setting: clinic
