id: mand
term: Mand
aliases: [request, demand, manding]
category: verbal-behavior
definition:
  technical: >-
    A verbal operant evoked by a motivating operation and reinforced by the specific
    thing it specifies — asking for water when thirsty and getting water. The mand is
    the only verbal operant that directly benefits the speaker, which is why it is
    usually the first one taught and why teaching it reduces problem behavior that was
    serving the same purpose.
  plain: >-
    Asking for something you want, and getting it. It is driven by wanting, and it is
    usually the first kind of language taught.
  gloss: A request, evoked by wanting and reinforced by getting it
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner signs "open" while holding a closed container and the adult opens it.
    setting: home
  - text: >-
      A student raises their hand and says "bathroom," and is let out.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner says "ball" when they see a ball and the adult says "yes, ball." Naming
      something present, for praise, is a tact.
    setting: home
