---
id: level
term: Level
category: graphing
definition:
  technical: >-
    The overall value of the data in a condition — roughly, how high or low the points
    sit on the vertical axis — often summarized by the mean or median of the phase. A
    change in level between phases is one of the main things visual analysis looks for.
  plain: >-
    How high or low the data sit on the graph. A jump from a high level to a low one
    is a change worth noticing.
  gloss: How high or low the data sit in a phase
examples:
  - text: >-
      Baseline data cluster around 12 per session; treatment data cluster around 3.
      The level dropped.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Data that start at 12 and fall steadily to 3 within one phase. That is a trend
      rather than a level change.
    setting: any
