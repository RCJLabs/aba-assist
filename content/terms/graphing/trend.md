---
id: trend
term: Trend
aliases: [slope, direction]
category: graphing
definition:
  technical: >-
    The overall direction of the data within a condition — increasing, decreasing or
    flat — and how steeply. A baseline trending in the direction the treatment is meant
    to produce cannot show that the treatment did anything, which is why baseline is
    extended until the trend is flat or moving the other way.
  plain: >-
    Which way the data are heading and how fast. If the behavior was already improving
    before treatment, treatment cannot take the credit.
  gloss: The direction the data are moving within a phase
examples:
  - text: >-
      Across ten baseline sessions the count climbs from 2 to 9. The trend is upward.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Data that jump between 2 and 9 from session to session with no direction. That is
      variability.
    setting: any
