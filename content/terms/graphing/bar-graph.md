---
id: bar-graph
term: Bar Graph
aliases: [histogram]
category: graphing
definition:
  technical: >-
    A display in which the height of a bar shows a summary value, such as the average
    or total per condition, per person or per category. It is useful for comparing
    discrete sets and for preference-assessment results, but because it collapses
    sessions into one value it cannot show trend or variability.
  plain: >-
    Bars whose height shows a total or an average. Good for comparing groups, bad for
    showing change over time.
  gloss: Bars showing summary values for comparison
examples:
  - text: >-
      One bar per item showing how often each was chosen in a preference assessment.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Daily data connected by lines across phases. That is a line graph.
    setting: clinic
