id: line-graph
term: Line Graph
aliases: [equal-interval line graph]
category: graphing
definition:
  technical: >-
    The standard display in behavior analysis: sessions or time on the horizontal axis,
    the measure of behavior on the vertical axis, data points connected within a
    condition, and vertical phase-change lines separating conditions. Points are not
    connected across phase lines, and the axes are labeled and scaled so that the size
    of a change is not exaggerated or hidden.
  plain: >-
    Dots for each session joined by lines, with a vertical line wherever the plan
    changed. It is how behavior data are usually shown.
  gloss: The standard graph of behavior over time
examples:
  - text: >-
      Daily counts of requests plotted across baseline and teaching phases, with a
      dashed line where teaching began.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A single bar per condition showing the average. That hides the session-by-session
      pattern the line graph exists to show.
    setting: clinic
