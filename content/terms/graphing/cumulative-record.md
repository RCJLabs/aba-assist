id: cumulative-record
term: Cumulative Record
aliases: [cumulative graph]
category: graphing
definition:
  technical: >-
    A graph in which each new data point adds the latest count to the running total, so
    the line can only go up or stay flat. Its slope shows rate: steep means fast
    responding, flat means none. It makes small changes in rate and the exact moment
    behavior stopped easy to see, and it was the original display of the experimental
    analysis of behavior.
  plain: >-
    A graph that keeps adding up the total, so the line never goes down. A steep line
    means the behavior is happening fast; a flat line means it stopped.
  gloss: A running total; the slope shows rate
examples:
  - text: >-
      A record of total words read across sessions, where the slope steepens after a
      new teaching method starts.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A graph of words read per session, which goes up and down from day to day. That is
      a standard line graph.
    setting: school
