---
id: variability
term: Variability
aliases: [bounce, stability]
category: graphing
definition:
  technical: >-
    How much the data points differ from one another within a condition. Low
    variability, or stability, makes changes between conditions easy to see; high
    variability can hide a real effect or fake one. Baseline is normally extended until
    the data are stable enough to predict from.
  plain: >-
    How much the data bounce around from session to session. When they bounce a lot,
    it is hard to tell whether anything changed.
  gloss: How much the data points differ within a phase
examples:
  - text: >-
      Session counts of 3, 11, 2, 14 and 5 in one phase. High variability.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Counts of 6, 7, 6, 7 and 6. That phase is stable.
    setting: any
