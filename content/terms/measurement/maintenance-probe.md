id: maintenance-probe
term: Maintenance Probe
aliases: [follow-up probe]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    A scheduled, unreinforced check of whether a mastered skill still occurs some time
    after teaching ended. Probes at increasing intervals are what turn maintenance from
    an assumption into a measure, and a skill that fails one is returned to teaching
    rather than recorded as lost.
  plain: >-
    A check, weeks after teaching stopped, of whether the skill is still there.
  gloss: A later check that a mastered skill has lasted
examples:
  - text: >-
      A skill mastered in March is probed once in April and again in June, with no
      prompts and no reinforcement.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Assuming a skill has maintained because it was mastered and nobody has reported a
      problem.
    setting: any
