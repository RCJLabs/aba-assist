term: Duration
aliases: [total duration, duration per occurrence]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    A continuous measure of how long a response lasts, recorded either as the total time
    across an observation or as the length of each separate occurrence. It suits behavior
    where how long it goes on matters more than how many times it starts.
  plain: >-
    How long a behavior lasts. You can record the total time across a session, or the
    length of each separate time it happens.
  gloss: How long the behavior lasts
examples:
  - text: A learner remained seated for eleven minutes of a fifteen-minute activity.

  - text: Staff timed each instance of hand washing to see whether it was long enough.
    setting: clinic

  - text: >-
      Counting how many times a learner left the table, which is frequency rather than
      duration.
    setting: clinic
  - text: >-
      Timing how long it took a learner to begin after an instruction. That gap is
      latency, not duration.

Choosing duration over frequency is a clinical decision that follows from what the goal
actually is. If a plan asks for duration, record duration even if counting feels easier.
