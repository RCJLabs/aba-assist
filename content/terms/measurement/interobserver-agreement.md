term: Interobserver Agreement
aliases: [IOA, reliability, interobserver reliability]
abbreviation: IOA
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The degree to which two independent observers recording the same events report the
    same result, usually expressed as a percentage. It is evidence that a measure reflects
    the behavior rather than the observer, and it is collected on a planned sample of
    sessions rather than all of them.
  plain: >-
    Two people watch the same session and record it separately, then compare. If they
    mostly agree, the data are probably about the learner and not about who was watching.
  gloss: Two observers recording the same session and comparing
examples:
  - text: >-
      Two staff independently counted requests during the same session and agreed on
      nineteen of twenty intervals.
    setting: clinic
  - text: A supervisor records alongside a technician once a month to check agreement.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Two staff filling in one shared sheet together. They are not independent, so this
      shows nothing about agreement.
    setting: clinic
  - text: >-
      One person recording the same session twice from memory, which measures that
      person's consistency rather than agreement.
    setting: any

Low agreement is information, not a failure. It usually means the definition of the
behavior is not tight enough yet, which is something to raise with your supervisor.
