id: continuous-measurement
term: Continuous Measurement
aliases: [continuous recording, event recording]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    Recording every instance of the target behavior during the observation period, so
    the record captures the full count, duration or timing rather than a sample. Count,
    rate, duration, latency and interresponse time are all continuous measures. It is
    the more accurate approach and the more demanding one for an observer who is also
    running a session.
  plain: >-
    Recording every single time the behavior happens, for the whole session. Most
    accurate, but hardest to do while teaching.
  gloss: Every instance of the behavior is recorded
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician tallies each request during a forty-minute session and times how
      long each tantrum lasts.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Checking every thirty seconds whether the learner is on task. That samples the
      behavior rather than recording every instance.
    setting: school
