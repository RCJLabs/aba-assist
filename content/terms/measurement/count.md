term: Count
aliases: [tally, number of occurrences]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The number of times a behavior occurred during an observation. On its own it is
    hard to compare across sessions of different lengths, which is why count is usually
    converted to rate by dividing by the observation time, or to a percentage by
    dividing by the number of opportunities.
  plain: >-
    How many times the behavior happened. To compare sessions of different lengths,
    turn it into a rate.
  gloss: The number of times a behavior occurred
examples:
  - text: >-
      Fourteen requests during the session.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Fourteen requests per hour. Dividing by time makes it a rate.
    setting: clinic
