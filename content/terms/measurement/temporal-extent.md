definition:
  technical: >-
    The property that every instance of behavior occupies some amount of time. It is what
    duration measures, and it is the property in play whenever the question is how long
    something went on rather than how many times it happened.
  plain: >-
    Behavior takes time. That is what lets you measure how long it lasted.

  - text: >-

  - text: >-
      A technician records that engagement with a task lasted 4 minutes 20 seconds across
      the session.

The reason this is worth separating from duration is that the property and the measure are
different things, and treating them as the same is what produces a data sheet that cannot
answer the question it was made for.

Choosing a measure is a two-step move: decide which property the concern is about, then
pick the quantity that measures it. A worry that a learner "takes ages to get started" is
about temporal locus, not extent, and a duration sheet will not show it.
