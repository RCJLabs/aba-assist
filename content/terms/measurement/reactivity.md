---
id: reactivity
term: Reactivity
aliases: [reactive effects of measurement]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    A change in behavior caused by the act of measuring it, such as a learner behaving
    better while an observer is visibly recording, or a staff member following the plan
    more closely during an integrity check. It usually fades with exposure, and it
    argues for unobtrusive measurement and for observing often enough that observation
    becomes ordinary.
  plain: >-
    People act differently when they know they are being watched or recorded. The
    effect usually wears off with time.
  gloss: Being observed changes the behavior being measured
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student's off-task behavior drops sharply the day a new observer sits at the
      back of the room, then returns over the week.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      An observer gradually loosening their scoring standard. That is observer drift.
    setting: clinic
