id: interresponse-time
term: Interresponse Time
aliases: [IRT]
abbreviation: IRT
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The time between the end of one response and the start of the next response of the
    same class. Short interresponse times mean a high rate; long ones a low rate. It is
    the measure that differential reinforcement of low rates is built on.
  plain: >-
    The gap between one behavior and the next one of the same kind. Short gaps mean it
    is happening fast.
  gloss: Time between two responses of the same kind
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner takes a bite, then another bite 40 seconds later. The interresponse
      time is 40 seconds.
    setting: home
  - text: >-
      A learner raises their hand, then raises it again four minutes later, and the gap
      between the two is what gets recorded.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The time from the instruction "take a bite" to the first bite. That is latency.
    setting: home
