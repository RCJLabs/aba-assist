id: generalization-probe
term: Generalization Probe
aliases: [generalisation probe]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    An unreinforced test of whether a skill occurs under conditions that were not used
    in teaching — a different person, setting, material or instruction. Probes are
    scheduled rather than improvised, and are run without prompts or reinforcement so
    that what they measure is the skill rather than the teaching arrangement.
  plain: >-
    A check of whether a skill works somewhere it was not taught, run without help or
    rewards so the result means something.
  gloss: An unreinforced test of a skill in untaught conditions
examples:
  - text: >-
      After mastery in the clinic, the learner is asked once at home by a parent, with
      no prompts and no reinforcement.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asking the learner to perform in the new setting while prompting them through it,
      which tests the prompt.
    setting: home
