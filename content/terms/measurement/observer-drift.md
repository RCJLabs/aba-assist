---
id: observer-drift
term: Observer Drift
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    A gradual, unintended change in how an observer applies a definition over time, so
    later data are scored by different standards than earlier data. It is a threat to
    accuracy that neither observer notices, and it is caught by periodic agreement
    checks and by retraining against the written definition.
  plain: >-
    Over time, an observer slowly starts to score the behavior in a new way. They do not
    notice it. Regular checks catch it.
  gloss: An observer's scoring standards slowly change
examples:
  - text: >-
      After weeks of recording, a technician starts counting brief taps as hits, which
      the definition never included.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner behaving differently because they know they are being watched. That
      change is reactivity, in the learner rather than the observer.
    setting: school
