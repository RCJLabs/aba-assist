id: mastery-criterion
term: Mastery Criterion
aliases: [acquisition criterion]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The stated performance standard at which a skill is treated as learned — a level of
    accuracy, across a number of sessions, under stated conditions. A criterion is a
    prediction that the skill will hold, so it is evaluated as well as applied: a
    pattern of mastery followed by loss at the maintenance check is evidence about the
    criterion rather than about the learner.
  plain: >-
    The rule that says when a skill counts as learned. A rule that keeps being followed
    by the skill disappearing is the wrong rule.
  gloss: The stated standard at which a skill counts as learned
examples:
  - text: >-
      Ninety per cent accuracy across three consecutive sessions with two different
      instructors, before the target moves to maintenance.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Treating a single perfect session as mastery, which cannot separate a learned
      skill from a good day.
    setting: any
