term: Magnitude
aliases: [intensity, force, amplitude]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The strength, force or intensity of a response — how loud, how hard, how far. It is
    measured directly when instruments allow (a decibel meter, a scale) and otherwise
    by a defined rating with anchors for each level, which needs agreement checks like
    any other measure.
  plain: >-
    How strong or intense a behavior is, such as how loud or how hard. Harder to measure
    well than how often.
  gloss: How intense or forceful a response is
