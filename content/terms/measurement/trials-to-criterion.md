id: trials-to-criterion
term: Trials to Criterion
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The number of response opportunities needed before the learner reaches a
    pre-set performance standard. It measures how quickly a skill is acquired and is
    useful for comparing teaching methods or tracking whether learning is speeding up
    across similar targets.
  plain: >-
    How many tries it takes to reach the goal. Fewer tries over time means the person
    is learning faster.
  gloss: Number of attempts needed to reach mastery
examples:
  - text: >-
      The first color took 60 trials to reach 90 percent correct; the fourth color took 15.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The percentage correct in a single session. That describes accuracy on one day,
      not how long mastery took.
    setting: clinic
