id: latency
term: Latency
aliases: [response latency]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The elapsed time between the onset of a stimulus, such as an instruction, and the
    beginning of the response that follows it. It measures how quickly responding starts,
    not how long it continues.
  plain: >-
    The wait between asking and the person starting. It measures how long it takes them
    to begin, not how long they keep going.
  gloss: The wait between the instruction and starting
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner began putting on their coat six seconds after being asked, so the latency
      was six seconds.
    setting: home
  - text: Staff recorded how long it took a learner to start work after each instruction.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Timing the whole task from start to finish, which is duration rather than the wait
      before beginning.
    setting: clinic
  - text: >-
      The gap between two separate responses by the same learner. That is
      interresponse time.
    setting: clinic

Latency often reveals progress that frequency hides. A learner who now starts in four
seconds instead of forty has changed a great deal, even if the number of responses is
the same.
