---
id: treatment-drift
term: Treatment Drift
aliases: [procedural drift]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    A gradual departure from the written procedure as an intervention continues, so
    that what is being delivered later differs from what was delivered at the start,
    often without anyone deciding to change it. It is a threat to internal validity and
    is detected by ongoing treatment-integrity measurement.
  plain: >-
    The plan slowly stops being followed exactly, a little at a time, without anyone
    deciding to change it. Regular integrity checks catch it.
  gloss: The procedure slowly changes from what was written
examples:
  - text: >-
      Over a month, prompts that were supposed to be delayed five seconds start coming
      almost immediately.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The supervisor formally revising the prompt delay and updating the plan. That is a
      planned change, not drift.
    setting: clinic
