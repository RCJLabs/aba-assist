id: permanent-product
term: Permanent Product
aliases: [product recording, outcome recording]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    Measuring a behavior by the lasting change it leaves in the environment rather than
    by observing the behavior itself — completed worksheets, a made bed, marks on a
    wall. It frees the observer from being present, but it is only valid when the
    product could not have been produced some other way.
  plain: >-
    Measuring what a behavior left behind instead of watching it happen. A finished
    worksheet shows the work was done.
  gloss: Behavior measured by the result it leaves behind
examples:
  - text: >-
      Counting the number of items put away in the bin after a clean-up routine.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Counting the number of times a learner raised a hand during a lesson. Nothing
      lasting is left to count afterward.
    setting: school
