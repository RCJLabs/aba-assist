id: percentage
term: Percentage
aliases: [percent correct, percent of opportunities, percent of intervals]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    A ratio of part to whole multiplied by 100 — correct responses over opportunities,
    or intervals with behavior over intervals observed. It is easy to read but hides the
    number of opportunities: 100% of two trials and 100% of twenty trials look the same,
    so the denominator should always be reported alongside it.
  plain: >-
    How much of the total, out of 100. Useful, but always ask "out of how many?"
  gloss: Part of a whole, out of 100
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner answered 8 of 10 questions correctly: 80 percent.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Twelve hits in a one-hour session. That is a count, and dividing by time gives a
      rate, not a percentage.
    setting: clinic
