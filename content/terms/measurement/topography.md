id: topography
term: Topography
aliases: [form of behavior]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The physical form of a behavior — what it looks like, the movements involved —
    as distinct from its function. Operational definitions usually describe topography
    because that is what an observer can see, but two behaviors with the same topography
    can serve different functions and the same function can be served by many
    topographies.
  plain: >-
    What a behavior looks like, the actual movements. It says nothing about why the
    behavior happens.
  gloss: The physical form of a behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      Defining hitting as an open hand making contact with another person's body from
      more than 15 cm away is a topographical description.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Describing the same behavior as hitting to get out of work. That names the
      function, not the form.
    setting: any
