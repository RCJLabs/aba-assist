id: treatment-integrity
term: Treatment Integrity
aliases: [procedural integrity, procedural fidelity, implementation fidelity]
category: measurement
definition:
  technical: >-
    The degree to which an intervention is carried out as it was designed, usually
    measured as the percentage of steps performed correctly during observed sessions.
    Low integrity makes outcome data uninterpretable: a plan that did not work may
    never have been delivered. It is also the measurement behind data-based decisions
    about retraining staff.
  plain: >-
    Whether the plan was actually done the way it was written. If it was not, the data
    cannot tell you whether the plan works.
  gloss: How closely the plan was followed as written
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor scores 9 of 10 program steps done correctly during an observation:
      90 percent integrity.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Two observers agreeing on how many hits occurred. That is interobserver agreement,
      which is about the data rather than the delivery of the plan.
    setting: clinic
