---
id: operational-definition
term: Operational Definition
aliases: [behavioral definition, target definition, observable and measurable]
category: documentation
definition:
  technical: >-
    A description of a behavior in terms of what can be seen and counted, stated precisely
    enough that two independent observers would agree on whether it occurred. It describes
    movement and form rather than inferred feelings, motives, or labels.
  plain: >-
    Describing a behavior by what you can see and count. It is written so clearly that two
    people watching would agree on whether it happened.
  gloss: Describing behavior by what can be seen and counted
examples:
  - text: >-
      Leaves the assigned area without permission and moves more than two metres from the
      table.
    setting: school
    why: Two observers could agree on this without guessing at intent.
  - text: Places head on the desk for longer than five continuous seconds during instruction.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Is defiant. Two observers would score this differently because it names a judgement
      rather than a movement.
    setting: any
  - text: >-
      Gets frustrated easily. Frustration cannot be observed directly, only guessed at from
      behavior.
    setting: any
