id: incident-report
term: Incident Report
aliases: [incident form, critical incident report]
category: documentation
definition:
  technical: >-
    The written record an organization requires after an unusual or serious event —
    an injury to anyone, a medical event, property damage, a restrictive procedure,
    an allegation. It is completed the same day, in objective language, by the person
    who was present, and it is separate from the session note. Employer policy, and
    often state regulation, set its form and deadline.
  plain: >-
    The form you fill in the same day when something serious or unusual happens, like
    an injury. Facts only, written by the person who was there.
  gloss: The same-day record of a serious or unusual event
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner trips and bruises a knee. The technician completes the incident form
      before leaving, noting the time, what happened, and who was told.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The routine note describing the day's programs and data. That is the session note.
    setting: clinic
