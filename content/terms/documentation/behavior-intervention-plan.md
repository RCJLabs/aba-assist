term: Behavior Intervention Plan
aliases: [BIP, behavior support plan, BSP, behavior plan, treatment plan]
abbreviation: BIP
category: documentation
definition:
  technical: >-
    The written plan, developed by the supervising analyst from assessment results,
    that specifies the target behaviors and their definitions, the hypothesized
    function, the antecedent strategies, the replacement behaviors to teach, the
    consequences for target and replacement behaviors, the data to collect, and the
    crisis procedures if any. Technicians implement it as written; changing it is the
    analyst's job.
  plain: >-
    The written plan for a person's behavior: what to change, why it happens, what to
    do before and after, what to teach instead, and what data to take. Technicians
    follow it; the analyst writes and changes it.
  gloss: The analyst's written plan for changing a behavior
examples:
  - text: >-
      A plan defines screaming, names escape as the likely function, adds a break card,
      states how staff respond, and lists the data sheet to use.
    setting: school
nonExamples:

      A list of classroom rules posted on the wall. It applies to everyone and rests on
      no assessment of an individual.
