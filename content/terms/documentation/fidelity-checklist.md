term: Fidelity Checklist
aliases: [integrity checklist, procedural checklist]
category: documentation
definition:
  technical: >-
    A written list of a procedure's steps, used to score whether it was delivered as
    designed. It is the instrument that turns procedural integrity from an impression
    into a measure, and it doubles as the feedback tool — which is why the steps have to
    be written as things an observer can see rather than as a summary of the procedure.
  plain: >-
    A list of a procedure's steps used to score whether somebody actually ran it as
    written.
  gloss: A step list used to score whether a procedure was run as written
examples:
  - text: >-
      An observer scores eleven of twelve steps as delivered and shows the one that was
      missed, which becomes the feedback.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Asking the staff member afterwards whether they followed the plan, which measures
      their report.
    setting: clinic
