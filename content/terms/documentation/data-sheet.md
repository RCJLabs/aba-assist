id: data-sheet
term: Data Sheet
aliases: [data collection sheet, datasheet, data form]
category: documentation
definition:
  technical: >-
    The form, paper or electronic, on which behavior is recorded during a session. A
    good one names the target and its definition, the measurement procedure, the date,
    the setting and the observer, and it is designed so recording does not interrupt
    teaching. It is a clinical record and is handled with the same confidentiality as
    any other.
  plain: >-
    The form where you record the behavior during a session. It should say what you
    are counting and how, and be quick to fill in while you work.
  gloss: The form behavior is recorded on during a session
examples:
  - text: >-
      A sheet with one row per trial, boxes for prompted and independent, and a space
      for the total at the end.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A note written from memory that evening. Recording after the fact is not data
      collection in the sense a plan requires.
    setting: any
