id: operant-conditioning
term: Operant Conditioning
aliases: [operant learning, instrumental conditioning]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Learning in which the future frequency of a behavior is changed by its consequences.
    Behavior that is followed by reinforcement becomes more likely; behavior followed by
    punishment or by no consequence becomes less likely. Almost everything a behavior
    plan does relies on operant relations.
  plain: >-
    Learning from what happens after you act. Behavior that pays off happens more;
    behavior that does not pay off happens less.
  gloss: Behavior is changed by its consequences
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who gets a laugh for a joke tells more jokes. The consequence changed
      the behavior.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner's eyes water when an onion is cut. That is a reflex, not something shaped
      by consequences.
    setting: home
