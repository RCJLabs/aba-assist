id: deprivation
term: Deprivation
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A period without access to a reinforcer, which increases its effectiveness and
    increases behavior that has produced it. It is the most common establishing
    operation, and restricting access to an item before a session — where ethically
    acceptable and within the plan — is one way to make it work as a reinforcer.
  plain: >-
    Going without something for a while makes it worth more. A person works harder for
    a thing they have not had recently.
  gloss: Going without a reinforcer raises its value
examples:
  - text: >-
      Bubbles are kept for sessions only. Because the learner does not get them at home,
      they stay effective during teaching.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who has just spent an hour on the tablet. Recent access lowers its value,
      which is satiation.
    setting: home
