id: conditioned-reinforcer
term: Conditioned Reinforcer
aliases: [secondary reinforcer, learned reinforcer]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A stimulus that has acquired the ability to reinforce behavior through a history of
    being paired with other reinforcers. Praise, tokens, money and a favorite song are
    conditioned reinforcers. If the pairing stops for long enough, the conditioned
    reinforcer can lose its effect.
  plain: >-
    Something that became a reward because it kept showing up with things that already
    were rewards. Praise and tokens are examples.
  gloss: A reinforcer whose power was learned through pairing
examples:
  - text: >-
      "Nice work" starts to increase a learner's effort after weeks of being paired
      with tickles and preferred toys.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A drink of water for a thirsty learner. Its power does not depend on any pairing.
    setting: any
