id: positive-punishment
term: Positive Punishment
aliases: [type I punishment]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A decrease in the future frequency of a behavior produced by the presentation of a
    stimulus immediately after it. "Positive" refers to something being added, not to
    anything pleasant. Like all punishment it is defined by its effect on behavior, and
    its use in practice is restricted, supervised and a last resort.
  plain: >-
    A behavior happens less because something was added right after it. Positive means
    added, not good.
  gloss: Behavior decreases because a stimulus was added
examples:
  - text: >-
      A child touches a hot stove, feels pain, and does not touch it again. The pain was
      added and the behavior dropped.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Losing screen time after hitting. Something was taken away, so if hitting drops
      this is negative punishment.
    setting: home
