id: abolishing-operation
term: Abolishing Operation
aliases: [AO]
abbreviation: AO
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A motivating operation that decreases the current effectiveness of a stimulus as a
    reinforcer and, correspondingly, decreases the current frequency of behavior that
    has produced that stimulus. Satiation is the usual example. Deliberately arranged,
    it is the basis of noncontingent reinforcement: giving free access to what a
    behavior used to earn makes the behavior less worth doing.
  plain: >-
    Something that makes a person want a thing less right now, so they stop working for
    it. Having plenty of something makes it matter less.
  gloss: Makes a reinforcer less valuable right now
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner given ten minutes of free attention before a session bids for attention
      far less during the session.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who has not had a drink all morning. Water is worth more, which is an
      establishing operation.
    setting: school
