id: establishing-operation
term: Establishing Operation
aliases: [EO]
abbreviation: EO
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A motivating operation that increases the current effectiveness of a stimulus as a
    reinforcer and, correspondingly, increases the current frequency of behavior that
    has produced that stimulus. Food deprivation is the classic example; so is being
    given a task that requires a tool you do not have.
  plain: >-
    Something that makes a person want a thing more right now, so they work harder for
    it. Being hungry makes food matter more.
  gloss: Makes a reinforcer more valuable right now
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner is handed a locked box with a favorite toy visible inside. The locked
      box makes asking for the key far more likely.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who has just finished a large lunch. Food is worth less now, which is an
      abolishing operation.
    setting: school
