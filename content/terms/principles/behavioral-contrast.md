id: behavioral-contrast
term: Behavioral Contrast
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A change in the rate of a behavior in one situation produced by a change in
    reinforcement in a different situation. Reducing reinforcement for a behavior in one
    setting can make it increase in another where reinforcement is unchanged. It is a
    reason plans need to be consistent across settings and people.
  plain: >-
    Changing what happens in one place changes the behavior somewhere else. Cutting
    off a behavior at school can make it go up at home.
  gloss: Behavior shifts in one setting when reinforcement changes in another
examples:
  - text: >-
      Attention for interrupting is withheld at school. The same week, interrupting at
      home rises even though nothing changed there.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A behavior rising in the same setting where reinforcement was withheld. That is
      an extinction burst.
    setting: any
