id: reinforcer
term: Reinforcer
aliases: [reinforcing stimulus, reward]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A stimulus change that, delivered as a consequence of a behavior, increases the
    future frequency of that behavior. The label is earned by effect, not by intention:
    an item is a reinforcer only if the data show the behavior it follows going up, and
    what reinforces one person's behavior may do nothing for another's.
  plain: >-
    Something that makes a behavior happen more when it follows that behavior. It only
    counts if it actually works.
  gloss: A consequence that makes behavior more likely
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who is handed the tablet after asking clearly asks clearly more often.
      For that behavior, the tablet is a reinforcer.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Stickers handed out after every task that the learner ignores, with no change in
      the behavior. Calling them a reward does not make them a reinforcer.
    setting: school
