id: maintenance
term: Maintenance
aliases: [response maintenance, durability]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The continuation of a behavior change after the intervention that produced it has
    been reduced or withdrawn. It is a form of generalization across time, and it has
    to be planned for — by thinning reinforcement, by handing the behavior over to
    naturally occurring reinforcers, and by checking with follow-up probes.
  plain: >-
    A skill still being used after the teaching has stopped. It has to be planned for;
    it does not happen on its own.
  gloss: Behavior change lasts after the intervention ends
examples:
  - text: >-
      Six months after a hand-washing program ended, probes show the learner still
      washes their hands after using the toilet.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A skill that is used only during sessions with the technician who taught it and
      never with anyone else. That is a generalization problem, not maintenance.
    setting: clinic
seeAlso: [generalization, schedule-thinning]
