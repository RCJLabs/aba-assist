term: Response Effort
aliases: [effort]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The physical or temporal cost of emitting a response, which reliably affects how
    often it occurs. Where two responses produce the same consequence, the one requiring
    less effort predominates — which makes effort one of the few determinants a
    practitioner can change directly, in either direction, without adding a consequence
    at all.
  plain: >-
    How much work a behaviour takes. Given two ways to get the same result, people take
    the easier one.
  gloss: How much work a response takes, which changes how often it occurs
