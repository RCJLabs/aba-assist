id: setting-event
term: Setting Event
aliases: [contextual variable, distal antecedent]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A condition or event, often removed in time from the behavior, that changes how a
    person responds to the immediate antecedents and consequences in a situation — a
    poor night's sleep, illness, a fight on the bus, a change in medication. Many
    setting events work as motivating operations, and plans often address them by
    adjusting expectations when they are present.
  plain: >-
    Something earlier in the day, or in the person's life, that makes a behavior more or
    less likely later. A bad night's sleep can change a whole session.
  gloss: An earlier condition that changes how a person responds now
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who missed breakfast is far more likely to protest work all morning.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      The instruction given right before a response. That is an immediate antecedent,
      not a setting event.
    setting: any
