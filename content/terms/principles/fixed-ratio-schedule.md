id: fixed-ratio-schedule
term: Fixed-Ratio Schedule
aliases: [FR schedule, FR]
abbreviation: FR
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A schedule in which reinforcement follows a set number of responses — FR 5 means
    every fifth response is reinforced. It typically produces a high, steady rate of
    responding with a pause after each reinforcer, and a ratio that is raised too
    quickly produces ratio strain, where responding breaks down.
  plain: >-
    A reward after a fixed number of responses, such as every fifth one. Behavior is
    fast, with a short break after each reward.
  gloss: Reinforcement after a fixed number of responses
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner earns a break after every three worksheets completed.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A break after an average of three worksheets, sometimes two, sometimes five. That
      is a variable ratio.
    setting: school
