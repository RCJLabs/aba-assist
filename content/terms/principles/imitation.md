id: imitation
term: Imitation
aliases: [motor imitation, generalized imitation]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A response that immediately follows a model's behavior, matches it in form, and is
    controlled by it — the model is the discriminative stimulus. Generalized imitation
    is the ability to copy new models without further training, and it is among the
    first skills targeted for many learners because so much later learning rides on it.
  plain: >-
    Copying what someone just did, right away and in the same form. Being able to copy
    new things without practice is a key early skill.
  gloss: Copying a model's behavior immediately after seeing it
examples:

      A learner who watches a sibling get a cookie for asking politely and asks
      politely the next day. Nothing was copied in the moment; that is observational
      learning.
