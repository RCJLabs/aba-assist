id: behavior
term: Behavior
aliases: [response, dead man's test]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Anything a living organism does that involves movement or a change in the
    relationship between the organism and its environment, including private events.
    The usual working test is that a dead person could not do it: "sitting quietly" or
    "not hitting" fail that test and describe the absence of behavior rather than
    behavior.
  plain: >-
    Anything a person does. If a dead person could do it, like "staying still," it is
    not behavior.
  gloss: What a person does; fails if a dead person could do it
examples:
  - text: A learner raises a hand, says a word, or walks to the door.
    setting: any
  - text: Thinking through a plan before acting is behavior too, though private.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      "Being calm" or "not screaming." These describe the absence of a response and
      cannot be measured as behavior on their own.
    setting: any
