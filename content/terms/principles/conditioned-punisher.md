id: conditioned-punisher
term: Conditioned Punisher
aliases: [secondary punisher, learned punisher]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A stimulus that has acquired the ability to decrease behavior through a history of
    being paired with other punishers. A stern "no" or a warning tone are common
    examples. Like conditioned reinforcers, they can lose their effect if the pairing
    stops, and they can become reinforcing instead if they reliably produce attention.
  plain: >-
    Something that came to reduce behavior because it was paired with things that
    already did. A firm "no" is a common example.
  gloss: A punisher whose effect was learned through pairing
examples:
  - text: >-
      A warning light on a machine reduces careless handling after workers learn it
      precedes a shock.
    setting: any
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Pain from touching a hot pan. Its effect on behavior needs no learning history.
    setting: home
