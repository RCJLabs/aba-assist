id: respondent-conditioning
term: Respondent Conditioning
aliases: [classical conditioning, Pavlovian conditioning, reflexive conditioning]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Learning in which a neutral stimulus comes to elicit a reflex response after being
    paired with a stimulus that already elicits it. The response is elicited by what
    comes before it, not maintained by what follows, and the learner does nothing to
    produce it. Respondent extinction occurs when the conditioned stimulus is presented
    repeatedly without the pairing.
  plain: >-
    Learning by pairing. A signal that used to mean nothing starts to trigger a reflex,
    like flinching or salivating, because it kept showing up with the real trigger.
  gloss: A neutral stimulus comes to elicit a reflex through pairing
