id: unconditioned-reinforcer
term: Unconditioned Reinforcer
aliases: [primary reinforcer, unlearned reinforcer]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A stimulus that functions as a reinforcer without any learning history because of
    the biology of the species — food when hungry, water when thirsty, warmth, sleep,
    relief from pain. Their effectiveness still rises and falls with motivating
    operations, so a primary reinforcer is not an always-on one.
  plain: >-
    Something that works as a reward from birth, like food or warmth. Nobody had to
    learn to want it.
  gloss: A reinforcer that needs no learning history
examples:
  - text: >-
      A small piece of a favorite snack delivered after a correct response to a hungry
      learner.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A token, which only works because it can be traded for something else.
    setting: clinic
