id: variable-ratio-schedule
term: Variable-Ratio Schedule
aliases: [VR schedule, VR]
abbreviation: VR
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A schedule in which reinforcement follows a number of responses that varies around
    an average — VR 5 means five responses on average, but any given reinforcer might
    come after two or nine. It produces high, steady responding with little pausing and
    is highly resistant to extinction. Slot machines run on it.
  plain: >-
    A reward after a number of responses that changes each time but averages out. Very
    steady behavior, and hard to stop.
  gloss: Reinforcement after a varying number of responses
examples:
  - text: >-
      Praise comes after the second correct answer, then the sixth, then the third,
      averaging about every fourth.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Praise after exactly every fourth answer, every time. That is a fixed ratio.
    setting: clinic
