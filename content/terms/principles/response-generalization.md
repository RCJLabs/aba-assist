id: response-generalization
term: Response Generalization
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The spread of the effects of training to responses that were not trained but serve
    the same function as the trained one. Teaching one way to greet may produce other
    greetings; teaching one way to solve a problem may produce related strategies.
  plain: >-
    You teach one behavior, and other behaviors that do the same job show up too. You
    never taught those extra ones.
  gloss: Untrained responses with the same function appear
examples:
  - text: >-
      Taught to say "hi," a learner starts waving and saying "hey" as well.
    setting: community
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Saying "hi" to new people after learning it with one person. The response is the
      same; the new stimuli make it stimulus generalization.
    setting: community
contrastWith: [stimulus-generalization]
