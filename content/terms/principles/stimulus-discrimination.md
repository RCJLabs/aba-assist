id: stimulus-discrimination
term: Stimulus Discrimination
aliases: [discrimination]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Responding differently to different stimuli — behaving in the presence of a
    discriminative stimulus and not in the presence of other stimuli. It is produced by
    reinforcing the behavior in the presence of one stimulus and withholding
    reinforcement in the presence of others, and it is the opposite side of the coin
    from stimulus generalization.
  plain: >-
    Telling stimuli apart and responding only to the right one. The opposite of
    treating similar things as the same.
  gloss: Responding differently to different stimuli
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner hands over the red block when asked for red and the blue block when
      asked for blue.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Handing over whichever block is closest regardless of the color named. No
      discrimination is in place yet.
    setting: clinic
