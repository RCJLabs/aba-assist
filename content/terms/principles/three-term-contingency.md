term: Three-Term Contingency
aliases: [ABC, antecedent-behavior-consequence, A-B-C contingency]
abbreviation: ABC
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The basic unit of operant analysis: an antecedent condition in whose presence a
    behavior occurs, and a consequence that follows and changes the future likelihood of
    that behavior in that condition. Antecedents come to control behavior only because
    of the consequences that have followed it in their presence.
  plain: >-
    Before, behavior, after. What was going on, what the person did, and what happened
    next. Most of ABA is working with those three parts.
  gloss: Antecedent, behavior, consequence
examples:
  - text: >-
      The teacher asks a question (antecedent), the student raises a hand (behavior),
      and the teacher calls on them (consequence).
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A list of a learner's behaviors with no note of what came before or after. That is
      a description of behavior, not a contingency.
    setting: any
