id: satiation
term: Satiation
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A reduction in the effectiveness of a reinforcer produced by recent or continued
    access to it, with a corresponding drop in behavior that has produced it. It is the
    usual reason a reinforcer that worked at the start of a session stops working by the
    end, and rotating reinforcers is the standard counter.
  plain: >-
    Having a lot of something recently makes it worth less. A reward that worked an
    hour ago may not work now.
  gloss: Recent access to a reinforcer lowers its value
examples:
  - text: >-
      After twenty small crackers, the learner no longer works for the next one.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who has not seen a favorite toy in a week and works hard for it. That
      is deprivation.
    setting: clinic
