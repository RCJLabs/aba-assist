id: continuous-reinforcement
term: Continuous Reinforcement
aliases: [CRF, FR1, fixed-ratio 1]
abbreviation: CRF
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A schedule in which every occurrence of the target response is reinforced. It is the
    fastest way to establish a new behavior and the least durable way to maintain one,
    because responding under continuous reinforcement extinguishes quickly once the
    reinforcement stops.
  plain: >-
    Every single time the behavior happens, it is rewarded. Best for teaching something
    new; not good for making it last.
  gloss: Every response is reinforced
examples:
  - text: >-
      During the first days of teaching a new request, every correct request produces the
      item.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Praising roughly every third correct answer. That is intermittent reinforcement.
    setting: school
