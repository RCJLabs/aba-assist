id: negative-punishment
term: Negative Punishment
aliases: [type II punishment]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A decrease in the future frequency of a behavior produced by the removal of a
    stimulus, usually a reinforcer, immediately after it. Response cost and time-out
    from reinforcement are the common procedural forms. As with all punishment, the
    definition is by effect on behavior.
  plain: >-
    A behavior happens less because something the person wanted was taken away. The
    loss comes right after the behavior.
  gloss: Behavior decreases because a stimulus was removed
examples:
  - text: >-
      A token is removed each time a student shouts out, and shouting out falls.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A reprimand after shouting out. Something was added, so if shouting falls this is
      positive punishment.
    setting: school
contrastWith: [positive-punishment]
