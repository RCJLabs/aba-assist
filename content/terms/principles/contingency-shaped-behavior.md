id: contingency-shaped-behavior
term: Contingency-Shaped Behavior
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Behavior established and maintained by direct contact with its consequences rather
    than by a rule describing them. It tends to be sensitive to changes in the actual
    contingencies and to be hard to describe in words even by the person doing it —
    riding a bicycle is the standard example.
  plain: >-
    Behavior learned by doing and finding out what happens, not by being told. Skills
    like balancing on a bike are learned this way.
  gloss: Behavior shaped directly by its consequences
examples:
  - text: >-
      A technician gradually learns the pace at which a particular learner stays engaged,
      without anyone stating it as a rule.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Following a written protocol step by step on the first day. That is rule-governed.
    setting: clinic
