id: premack-principle
term: Premack Principle
aliases: [grandma's law, first-then]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The finding that access to a more probable behavior can reinforce a less probable
    one. Instead of asking what item a person likes, it asks what the person does a lot
    when free to choose, and makes that activity contingent on the target. "First work,
    then play" is its everyday form.
  plain: >-
    Something a person does a lot can be used as a reward for something they do less.
    First the work, then the fun thing.
  gloss: A high-probability activity reinforces a low-probability one
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who spends free time drawing earns drawing time by finishing a page of
      reading.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Giving several easy instructions before a hard one to build compliance. That is a
      high-probability request sequence, which is about momentum rather than a reward.
    setting: school
