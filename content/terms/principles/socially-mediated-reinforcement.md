id: socially-mediated-reinforcement
term: Socially Mediated Reinforcement
aliases: [social reinforcement, mediated consequence]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Reinforcement that is delivered by another person as a consequence of the behavior:
    attention, help, access to items, or removal of a demand. Three of the four
    commonly assessed functions of behavior are socially mediated, which is why they can
    be tested by changing what other people do.
  plain: >-
    When another person provides the reward, such as attention, a toy, or a break. The
    behavior works because of what other people do next.
  gloss: Another person delivers the consequence
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student calls out and the teacher answers. The answer is the socially mediated
      consequence.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner spins a wheel for the visual effect when no one is present. The
      consequence is automatic, not social.
    setting: home
