id: spontaneous-recovery
term: Spontaneous Recovery
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The reappearance of a behavior that had been reduced to low levels by extinction,
    after a period of time in which it did not occur, without any renewed reinforcement.
    It is usually brief and weaker than the original level, and it disappears again if
    extinction is maintained.
  plain: >-
    A behavior that had stopped shows up again for a while, even though nothing rewarded
    it. If the plan stays in place, it fades again.
  gloss: An extinguished behavior briefly returns
examples:
  - text: >-
      Two weeks after tantrums stopped, one tantrum happens on a Monday morning and
      then none the rest of the week.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Tantrums getting louder in the first days after attention was withheld. That is
      the extinction burst.
    setting: home
