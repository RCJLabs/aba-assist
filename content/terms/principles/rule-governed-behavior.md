id: rule-governed-behavior
term: Rule-Governed Behavior
aliases: [verbally governed behavior, instruction following]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Behavior controlled by a verbal description of a contingency — a rule, instruction
    or self-statement — rather than by direct contact with the consequences the rule
    describes. It lets people act on consequences that are delayed, improbable or
    dangerous to contact, and it can also persist when the rule is wrong or the
    contingency has changed.
  plain: >-
    Doing something because of a rule or instruction, not because you have experienced
    the result yourself. It is fast to learn but can be hard to update.
  gloss: Behavior controlled by a stated rule
examples:
  - text: >-
      A new technician wears gloves for a procedure because the policy says to, before
      ever seeing what happens without them.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A child learns which drawer sticks by pulling on drawers. No rule is involved; the
      behavior was shaped by its consequences.
    setting: home
