id: variable-interval-schedule
term: Variable-Interval Schedule
aliases: [VI schedule, VI]
abbreviation: VI
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A schedule in which the first response after a varying, unpredictable amount of
    time is reinforced, with the intervals averaging a set value. It produces moderate,
    steady responding without the pause-and-burst pattern of a fixed interval, and it
    is a common choice for maintaining behavior in natural settings.
  plain: >-
    After an amount of time that changes unpredictably, the next response is rewarded.
    Behavior stays moderate and steady.
  gloss: First response after a varying time is reinforced
examples:
  - text: >-
      A supervisor drops in at unpredictable times and praises staff found running the
      program correctly.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Praise delivered on a timer every ten minutes whether or not the behavior
      happened. With no response requirement, that is a time-based schedule.
    setting: clinic
