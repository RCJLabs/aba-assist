term: Resurgence
aliases: [treatment relapse, renewal, reinstatement]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The return of a previously reinforced behavior when the behavior that replaced it
    stops being reinforced. It is one of several forms of relapse — along with renewal,
    where behavior returns when the context changes, and reinstatement, where behavior
    returns after the old reinforcer is delivered again — and it matters most when a
    plan thins reinforcement for a replacement behavior too quickly.
  plain: >-
    An old problem behavior comes back when the new behavior stops paying off. It shows
    why the replacement behavior has to keep working.
  gloss: Old behavior returns when the new behavior stops working
examples:
  - text: >-
      A learner taught to ask for a break stops getting breaks when the new teacher
      does not know the plan. Within days the old escape behavior is back.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A behavior briefly returning weeks into extinction with no change in the
      replacement. That is spontaneous recovery.
    setting: any
