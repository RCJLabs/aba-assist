id: concurrent-schedule
term: Concurrent Schedule
aliases: [concurrent schedules of reinforcement, choice arrangement]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Two or more schedules operating at the same time for two or more different
    behaviors, so the person can choose which to engage in. Most everyday situations are
    concurrent schedules, and how behavior is allocated among the options follows the
    relative rate, quality, delay and effort of reinforcement on each — the subject of
    the matching law.
  plain: >-
    Two or more options available at the same time, each with its own payoff. The
    person chooses between them.
  gloss: Two or more schedules available at once
examples:
  - text: >-
      A student can work quietly for occasional praise or joke with a neighbor for
      frequent laughs. Both are available at once.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A schedule in which praise alternates with tokens for the same behavior. One
      response class, alternating conditions, is a multiple schedule.
    setting: school
