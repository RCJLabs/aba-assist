id: s-delta
term: S-Delta
aliases: [SΔ, S-delta stimulus, extinction stimulus]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A stimulus in whose presence a behavior has not been reinforced, so the behavior
    occurs less often when it is present. It is the counterpart of the discriminative
    stimulus: together they define a discrimination.
  plain: >-
    A signal that a behavior will not pay off right now. The person learns to hold off
    when it is present.
  gloss: A stimulus that signals reinforcement is unavailable
examples:
  - text: >-
      A "closed" sign on the snack cupboard. Asking for a snack while it is up has never
      produced one, so asking stops when the sign is up.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A green card that means help is available. That signals reinforcement, which
      makes it a discriminative stimulus.
    setting: school
