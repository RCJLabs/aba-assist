id: compound-schedule
term: Compound Schedule
aliases: [complex schedule]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A schedule built from two or more simple schedules. They differ in whether the
    component schedules run at the same time or in sequence, whether each component is
    signaled by its own stimulus, and whether reinforcement is delivered after each
    component or only at the end. Multiple (signaled, alternating), mixed (unsignaled,
    alternating), chained (signaled, sequential) and tandem (unsignaled, sequential)
    are the commonly named forms.
  plain: >-
    Two or more simple schedules combined. They can run one after another or at the
    same time, and each part may or may not have its own signal.
  gloss: Two or more simple schedules combined
examples:
  - text: >-
      A morning routine where each step is signaled by a picture and the reinforcer
      comes only at the end is a chained schedule.
    setting: home
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A single FR 3 schedule running on its own. One simple schedule is not a compound.
    setting: any
