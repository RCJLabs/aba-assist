id: generalized-conditioned-reinforcer
term: Generalized Conditioned Reinforcer
aliases: [generalized reinforcer]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    A conditioned reinforcer that has been paired with many different reinforcers and so
    works under a wide range of motivating conditions. Money and tokens are the standard
    examples: their value does not depend on the learner being deprived of any one thing.
  plain: >-
    A learned reward that works almost any time, because it can be swapped for many
    different things. Money and tokens are the classic examples.
  gloss: A learned reinforcer backed by many other reinforcers
examples:
  - text: >-
      Tokens that can be traded for snacks, games, or a walk keep working even after the
      learner has just eaten.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A token that can only ever be exchanged for one specific toy. Once the learner is
      tired of the toy, the token stops working.
    setting: clinic
