category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    The emergence of untrained relations among stimuli after a few relations have been
    taught. If a learner is taught that A goes with B and B goes with C, equivalence is
    shown when they also match B to A and C to B (symmetry), A to C (transitivity), and
    each stimulus to itself (reflexivity) without training. It is the basis of
    equivalence-based instruction and a behavioral account of meaning.
  plain: >-
    Teach a few links between things and the learner works out the other links on their
    own. Teach that a word goes with a picture and a picture with a written word, and
    the word and written word connect too.
  gloss: Untrained relations emerge from trained ones
examples:
  - text: >-
      Taught to match the spoken word "three" to the numeral 3, and the numeral to three
      dots, a learner then matches the spoken word to three dots with no teaching.
    setting: school
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      A learner who has to be taught every pairing separately and shows nothing new.
      The relations were trained, not derived.
    setting: school
