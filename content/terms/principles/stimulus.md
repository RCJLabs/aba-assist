id: stimulus
term: Stimulus
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    An event, object or condition in the environment that can affect behavior — a
    sound, a sight, a touch, a person entering the room, or a change in temperature.
    Stimuli are described by their physical features and by their function, and the same
    physical event can serve different functions for different people or at different
    times.
  plain: >-
    Anything in the surroundings that can affect what a person does. A sound, an object,
    a person, a change in the room.
  gloss: An environmental event that can affect behavior
examples:
  - text: A timer beeping, a teacher's instruction, or a cold draft from a window.
