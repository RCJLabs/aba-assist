id: intermittent-reinforcement
term: Intermittent Reinforcement
aliases: [INT, partial reinforcement]
category: principles
definition:
  technical: >-
    Any schedule in which only some occurrences of the response are reinforced. Behavior
    maintained intermittently is more resistant to extinction than behavior maintained
    continuously, which is why plans thin to intermittent schedules for maintenance —
    and why a problem behavior that sometimes works is so persistent.
  plain: >-
    Only some of the behaviors are rewarded, not every one. This makes behavior last
    longer, for good behavior and for problem behavior alike.
  gloss: Only some responses are reinforced
examples:
  - text: >-
      After a skill is learned, praise is delivered for about every fourth correct
      response instead of every one.
    setting: clinic
nonExamples:
  - text: >-
      Reinforcing every correct response during the first teaching sessions. That is
      continuous reinforcement.
    setting: clinic
