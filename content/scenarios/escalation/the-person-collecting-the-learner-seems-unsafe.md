title: The person collecting the learner seems unsafe
situation: >-
  The session is ending and the adult who has come for the learner seems impaired, is
  behaving in a way that alarms you, or is somebody you have not been told about and cannot
  identify. Handing the learner over feels wrong. Refusing feels like something you have no
  authority to do.
askedAs:
  - seems drunk at pickup
  - smells of alcohol at collection
  - person collecting them seems impaired
  - stranger came to collect the learner
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: [suspected-abuse]
escalation:
  stopAndEscalate: true
  contacts:
    - supervising-bcba
    - site-supervisor
    - child-protective-services
    - parent-guardian
  immediateSafetyNote: >-
    Keep the learner with you and in sight while you make a call. You do not have to
    resolve this by yourself and you do not have to announce a judgment to the person in
    front of you — a delay of a few minutes while you reach your supervisor is reasonable,
    ordinary, and far easier to defend afterwards than a handover you were uneasy about.
  mandatedReporterNote: >-
    If you believe a child or vulnerable adult is being placed in danger, the mandated
    reporting duty applies here as it does anywhere else, on reasonable suspicion rather
    than proof. Which agency and how long you have are set by your state.
  legalNote: >-
    Who may collect a learner is set by the family, the placing authority and your
    employer, and a custody arrangement you have not been shown may be the reason
    somebody is or is not on the list. That is exactly why this goes up rather than being
    decided at the door.
  documentation:
    - Record the time, who arrived, and what you observed that concerned you.
    - Describe behavior rather than labeling a state, so what you saw and heard is what is on record.
    - Record who you contacted, when, and what you were told to do.
    - Record what actually happened at the end, including who left with the learner.
  consultYourPolicy: true
