title: The learner says they want to die or hurt themselves
situation: >-
  A learner — of any age — says, writes, signs or otherwise communicates that they want
  to die, that they wish they were dead, or that they plan to hurt themselves. It may be
  said calmly or in distress, and it may be the first time or one of many.
askedAs:
  - said they want to die
  - said they wish they were dead
  - talking about killing themself
  - said everyone would be better off without them
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: [suicidal-ideation]
escalation:
  stopAndEscalate: true
  contacts:
    - crisis-line-988
    - emergency-services-911
    - supervising-bcba
    - parent-guardian
    - site-supervisor
  immediateSafetyNote: >-
    Take it seriously every time. Stay with the learner, listen without arguing or
    minimizing, and do not leave them alone. In the US, the 988 Suicide and Crisis
    Lifeline can be called or texted for guidance in the moment, and emergency services
    if there is immediate danger. Your organization's crisis procedure says who to call
    and in what order.
  mandatedReporterNote: null
  legalNote: >-
    Assessing risk is outside a technician's scope and outside this app's. The
    supervising analyst, the family, and where needed a qualified mental-health
    professional decide what happens next. Your employer's policy governs the steps you
    take.
  documentation:
    - Record what the learner communicated, in their own words as closely as you can, with the time.
    - Record what was happening before, who was present, and how the learner seemed.
    - Record who you contacted, when, and what they told you to do.
    - Complete your organization's incident or critical-event report the same day.
  consultYourPolicy: true
