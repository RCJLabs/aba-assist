title: The learner has hit, bitten or kicked someone and there is an injury
situation: >-
  During a session the learner strikes, bites, kicks or throws something at another
  person — you, a peer, a family member — and that person is hurt, or you cannot yet
  tell whether they are. The behavior may or may not be continuing.
askedAs:
  - bit me and drew blood
  - someone was injured during the incident
  - hit another child and they are hurt
  - kicked a staff member who is injured
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: [aggression-with-injury]
escalation:
  stopAndEscalate: true
  contacts:
    - nurse-or-medical
    - emergency-services-911
    - site-supervisor
    - supervising-bcba
    - parent-guardian
  immediateSafetyNote: >-
    Get help from another adult immediately, attend to the injured person, and call
    emergency services if the injury could be serious. What staff may do to keep the
    learner and others safe is set by the learner's plan, your site's crisis procedure and
    your certified training, and by nothing else.
  mandatedReporterNote: null
  legalNote: >-
    Physical management is governed by employer policy, certified training and state
    law. Any injury to a client or a staff member is reported to your supervisor the
    same day, however minor, and most employers require a written incident report.
  documentation:
    - Record what happened immediately before, what the learner did, and what happened immediately after, using objective language.
    - Record every injury, to whom, and what care was given.
    - Record who you contacted and when.
    - Complete the incident report and any injury forms your employer requires before leaving for the day.
  consultYourPolicy: true
