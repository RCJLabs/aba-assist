title: The learner has a seizure, stops breathing normally, or collapses
situation: >-
  During a session the learner has a seizure, has trouble breathing, chokes, faints,
  has a severe allergic reaction, or shows any other sign of a medical emergency. You
  may or may not know their medical history.
askedAs:
  - having a seizure
  - stopped breathing properly
  - collapsed and will not wake
  - choking on something
  - lips turning blue
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: [medical-emergency]
escalation:
  stopAndEscalate: true
  contacts:
    - emergency-services-911
    - nurse-or-medical
    - parent-guardian
    - supervising-bcba
  immediateSafetyNote: >-
    Call emergency services, or have someone call while you stay with the learner, and
    follow the learner's medical or seizure plan if there is one and you have been trained
    in it. First aid is a certified skill; do what you are trained to do and no more, and
    let the dispatcher guide you.
  mandatedReporterNote: null
  legalNote: >-
    Medical response is governed by the learner's medical plan, your first-aid training
    and certification, and employer policy. A behavior plan never overrides a medical
    one.
  documentation:
    - Record the time the event started and ended, and what you observed.
    - Record when emergency services were called and when they arrived.
    - Record who was present and who was notified.
    - Complete the incident report the same day and tell your supervisor.
  consultYourPolicy: true
