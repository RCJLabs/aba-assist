title: A learner is breaking things and someone could be hurt
situation: >-
  The learner is throwing furniture, pulling things off walls, breaking glass or damaging
  equipment, and the room is no longer a safe place for the people in it. There are other
  learners nearby, or there is nobody else at all and you are on your own with it.
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: [property-destruction-danger]
escalation:
  stopAndEscalate: true
  contacts:
    - supervising-bcba
    - site-supervisor
    - agency-safety-officer
  immediateSafetyNote: >-
    Move the people, not the learner. Clear everybody else out of the room, put distance
    between the learner and anything that could injure somebody, and call for whatever help
    your site's protocol names. Property can be replaced and the session can be abandoned;
    neither is a reason to put a person between a learner and a thrown object.
  mandatedReporterNote: null
  legalNote: >-
    What staff may do in this situation is set by your employer's crisis protocol, the
    learner's written plan and state law, in that combination and no other. An instruction
    given in the moment does not override any of them, and if what you are being asked to
    do requires a certification you do not hold, the answer is that you do not hold it.
  documentation:
    - Record what happened, in order, and what was damaged.
    - Record what preceded it, as an observation rather than an explanation.
    - Record who was present, who was moved where, and who you called.
    - Complete the incident form your employer requires, the same day.
    - Report it to your supervising analyst even where nobody was injured.
  consultYourPolicy: true
