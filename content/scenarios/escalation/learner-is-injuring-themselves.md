This app will not tell you what to do physically here, and that is deliberate rather than
an omission.

Physical management is a certified, hands-on competency taught by programs such as
Safety-Care, CPI, and PRO-ACT. It is assessed in person and renewed on a schedule. It
cannot be learned from a screen, and an app cannot know whether you have been trained,
what this learner's plan authorizes, or what your state permits. Survey research finds
that a substantial share of technicians receive no training for situations like this one,
which is a reason for the field to fix its training, not a reason for a reference app to
improvise instructions.

If you have not been trained in your employer's crisis procedure, say so to your
supervisor before you are alone with a learner who has a history of these situations.
