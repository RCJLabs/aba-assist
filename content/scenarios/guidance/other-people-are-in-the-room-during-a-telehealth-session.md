title: Other people are within earshot of a telehealth session
situation: >-
  During a video session you realise somebody else is in the room or nearby — a sibling,
  a relative, a neighbour — and they can hear what is being said. You may also be
  somewhere others can hear your side of it.
setting: telehealth
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: >-
      Check your own side first. Use headphones, close the door, and make sure nobody can
      see your screen.
    rationale: >-
      The half of the call you control is the half you are responsible for, and it is the
      easier half to fix.
  - text: >-
      Tell the caregiver what you have noticed, plainly and without blame.
    rationale: >-
      Families are usually managing a household rather than making a decision about privacy.
      Naming it lets them choose.
  - text: >-
      Keep going if the caregiver is content and the plan does not require privacy. Adjust
      what you say out loud if it does.
    rationale: >-
      Whose privacy is at stake is the family's call within the limits of the plan, not an
      independent judgement for the technician.
  - text: >-
      Avoid saying anything about the learner's progress, difficulties or history that you
      would not say in front of them.
    rationale: >-
      Anything audible is effectively said to everybody in the room, and that includes the
      learner.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not run the session somewhere your own household can overhear it.
  - >-
    Do not record the session on your own device.
  - >-
    Do not discuss the learner with whoever else is present, even if they ask.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor if the setting makes it impossible to run the session as the plan
    requires.
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor the same day if you think confidential information reached
    somebody who should not have heard it.
