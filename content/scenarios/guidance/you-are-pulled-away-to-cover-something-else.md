title: You are asked to cover another task during your session time
situation: >-
  A member of school staff asks you to cover a class, supervise elsewhere, or help with
  something unrelated during the time you are scheduled with the learner. The request is
  reasonable from their side and the school is short-staffed.
setting: school
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: >-
      Say what you are scheduled to be doing, and who scheduled it.
    rationale: >-
      The person asking often does not know that your time is allocated clinically rather
      than by the school timetable.
  - text: >-
      Do not agree to miss a session on your own authority.
    rationale: >-
      A session is part of a programme somebody is accountable for. Whether it can be
      dropped is not a decision made in a corridor.
  - text: >-
      Offer what you can do that does not cost the session, such as helping afterwards.
    rationale: >-
      Declining the request while offering something else keeps the working relationship
      intact, and the relationship matters.
  - text: >-
      Tell your supervisor the same day, whatever the outcome.
    rationale: >-
      If sessions are being lost, that is a pattern only the supervisor can address with the
      school.
  - text: >-
      Record any session that did not happen, and why.
    rationale: >-
      Missed sessions that leave no trace are invisible, and the programme looks as though
      it simply stopped working.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not work outside what you have been trained and are supervised to do.
  - >-
    Do not agree to a standing arrangement to cover other duties.
  - >-
    Do not record the session as delivered if it was not.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor the same day if you were asked to miss a session.
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor if the request comes repeatedly, because it needs settling
    between the school and your employer.
