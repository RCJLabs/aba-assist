id: a-reinforcer-stops-working-during-the-session
kind: guidance
title: The reinforcer that worked at the start of the session stops working
situation: >-
  For the first twenty minutes the learner worked steadily for a preferred item. Now the
  same item produces no interest. The learner is not upset and nothing is unsafe; they
  are simply no longer working for what is on offer.
setting: clinic
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: Assume satiation before assuming the program has stopped working.
    rationale: >-
      Recent access lowers the value of almost any reinforcer. This is the most likely
      explanation and the least alarming one.
  - text: Offer a choice from the reinforcers listed in the program, if the program allows choice.
    rationale: >-
      Choice lets the learner tell you what is valuable right now, and rotating items
      counters satiation.
  - text: Run the brief preference check if one is written into the program, and use its result.
    rationale: >-
      A quick preference assessment at the point of need is exactly what such procedures
      are for.
  - text: Note in your session record which reinforcers were used and when interest dropped.
    rationale: >-
      Patterns across sessions tell the supervisor whether to expand the reinforcer menu
      or change the schedule.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not bring in something that is not on the program's list, including food, without
    approval.
  - >-
    Do not push through with the same item; trials without an effective reinforcer are
    practice at not responding.
  - >-
    Do not restrict the learner's access to the item outside sessions on your own
    initiative; that decision belongs to the supervisor and the family.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor if nothing on the list works for more than a session or two, or
    if the drop in interest is sudden and unusual for this learner.
