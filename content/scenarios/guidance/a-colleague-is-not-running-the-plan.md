id: a-colleague-is-not-running-the-plan
kind: guidance
title: A colleague is not running the plan as written
situation: >-
  You see another technician working with the same learner and they are doing it
  differently — skipping a step, prompting differently, giving reinforcement the plan
  does not specify. Nobody is at risk. It is a treatment-integrity problem, not a safety
  one.
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: >-
      Check the plan before you act, in case they are right and you are the one who has
      drifted.
    rationale: >-
      Two people can both believe they are running it as written. The document settles it,
      and being wrong about this is common.
  - text: >-
      If it is a small difference and the relationship allows it, mention it directly and
      without weight.
    rationale: >-
      Most differences are drift rather than disagreement, and a colleague usually wants to
      know.
  - text: >-
      Tell your supervisor either way, describing what you saw rather than what you
      concluded.
    rationale: >-
      Consistency across everybody who runs the programme is what the supervisor is
      accountable for, and they cannot fix what they cannot see.
  - text: >-
      Keep running the plan as written yourself.
    rationale: >-
      Matching a colleague to keep the sessions consistent makes two people wrong and
      removes the only correct record.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not retrain your colleague on the plan yourself.
  - >-
    Do not raise it in front of the learner or the family.
  - >-
    Do not change how you run it to match theirs.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor whenever the plan is being run differently by different people.
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor the same day if the difference could affect the learner rather
    than only the data.
