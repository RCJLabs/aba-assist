id: you-are-unwell-before-a-session
kind: guidance
title: You are unwell and have a session booked
situation: >-
  You wake up ill. The session is today. You know the learner does better with a
  consistent person, you may not be paid if you do not work, and cancelling feels like
  letting the family down.
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: >-
      Follow your employer's sickness policy first. It is the rule that actually governs
      this.
    rationale: >-
      Whether you work is not only your decision. Employers and settings have rules, and
      some are legal requirements rather than preferences.
  - text: >-
      Tell your supervisor and the scheduler as early as you can.
    rationale: >-
      Early notice is the difference between a covered session and a family waiting at the
      door.
  - text: >-
      Do not weigh the learner's consistency against an illness you could pass on.
    rationale: >-
      Working through it protects the schedule and risks the person you are there for, and
      many of the people this app serves work with medically vulnerable learners.
  - text: >-
      If you are well enough to work but not to work safely with this learner, say exactly
      that.
    rationale: >-
      The useful report is what you can and cannot do today, not a verdict on whether you
      are ill.
  - text: >-
      Record the missed session the way your employer requires.
    rationale: >-
      A missing session with no record looks like a programme that quietly stopped.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not decide on your own that you are well enough because the family is expecting
    you.
  - >-
    Do not arrange your own replacement outside the normal process.
  - >-
    Do not work while unwell to protect your hours.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor as soon as you know you cannot work.
  - >-
    Ask your supervisor if you are unsure whether your condition is one that should keep
    you away.
