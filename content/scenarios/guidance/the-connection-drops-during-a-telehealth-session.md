title: The connection fails partway through a telehealth session
situation: >-
  You are running a session over video and the connection drops, freezes badly, or the
  audio stops. You cannot see or hear the learner reliably, and you do not know whether
  they can see or hear you.
setting: telehealth
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: Try to reconnect once, and if the platform has a backup — a phone number, a second link — use it.
    rationale: >-
      A short interruption that is repaired is a different event from a session that ended,
      and it should not be recorded as the same thing.
  - text: If you cannot get back within the time your employer specifies, contact the caregiver by the agreed means.
    rationale: >-
      Somebody at the other end is waiting, and they do not know whether the problem is
      theirs.
  - text: Stop collecting data for the period you could not observe.
    rationale: >-
      Data recorded for minutes you could not see are not data. They are a guess that will be
      read later as a measurement.
  - text: Write up what you did run, when the connection failed, and how long was lost.
    rationale: >-
      The session note is what the supervisor and the funder both work from, and an
      unexplained short session raises questions the note should already answer.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not fill in the missed interval from what you expect would have happened.
  - >-
    Do not carry on scoring if you can hear but not see, or see but not hear, unless the
    measure allows it.
  - >-
    Do not switch to a personal messaging app to keep going.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell your supervisor the same day if a scheduled session could not be delivered.
  - >-
    Raise it with your supervisor if connection failures are frequent, because it is a
    treatment-integrity problem, not just an inconvenience.
