kind: guidance
title: The learner is being left out by the other children
situation: >-
  At break, or during a group activity, the other children are not including the
  learner. Nobody is being unkind in a way the school would act on. The learner is
  simply on their own, and appears to want not to be.
setting: school
audience: [RBT, BCaBA, BCBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: >-
      Check whether the plan has social targets, and run those rather than improvising.
    rationale: >-
      Social skills are commonly programmed. If they are, there is already an agreed way to
      arrange this that will produce usable data.
  - text: >-
      Arrange the situation rather than instructing the children. Put the learner near an
      activity they can join, with materials that invite more than one person.
    rationale: >-
      Changing what is available is an antecedent change, and it is within what a technician
      runs. Directing other children is not.
  - text: >-
      Reinforce the learner's approaches to peers in the way the plan specifies, and keep
      your own involvement light.
    rationale: >-
      An adult standing over an interaction changes it. The goal is time with peers, not
      time with you near peers.
  - text: >-
      Record what you saw, including what the other children did.
    rationale: >-
      Whether this is a skill to teach, a setting to change, or both, is decided from
      observation rather than impression.
whatNotToDo:
  - >-
    Do not tell other children to play with the learner.
  - >-
    Do not explain the learner's difficulties to classmates to win sympathy.
  - >-
    Do not fill the whole break yourself so that the learner is not alone.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Tell the teacher and your supervisor if the learner is being excluded deliberately or
    persistently.
  - >-
    Raise it with your supervisor if there is no social programming and you think there
    should be.
