id: you-are-scheduled-without-the-behavior-plan
kind: guidance
title: You are scheduled with a learner whose plan you have never seen
situation: >-
  You have been put on a session — a cover shift, a new client, a reassignment — and
  nobody has given you the program, the data sheets or the written plan. You are
  expected to start in an hour, and the person who scheduled you is treating it as routine.
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: Ask for the written plan and the programs before the session, in writing, from whoever scheduled you.
    rationale: >-
      Asking in writing costs nothing and means the request exists afterwards. Most of the
      time the documents are simply sitting somewhere nobody thought to send.
  - text: Say plainly which parts you have not been trained on for this learner, if any.
    rationale: >-
      Delivering only what you have been trained and assessed on is a requirement of the
      code, not a preference, and the person scheduling you may not know what you have been
      shown.
  - text: If the documents do not arrive, ask what you should do in the session and get the answer in writing.
    rationale: >-
      "Just do what you normally do" is not a plan for a learner you do not know, and
      asking converts an assumption into an instruction somebody has taken responsibility for.
  - text: Where you do go ahead without the plan, keep to ordinary interaction and general reinforcement, record what happened, and change nothing.
    rationale: >-
      A session where nothing was run is a session with no data. A session where the wrong
      procedure was run is worse, because the data look real.
  - text: Send a short written note afterwards saying what you did and did not run.
    rationale: >-
      It tells the analyst how to read the day's data, which is the difference between a
      gap and a false record.
whatNotToDo:
  - Do not guess at a procedure because you have run something similar with another learner.
  - Do not run a behavior reduction procedure you have not been trained on for this learner, under any circumstances.
  - Do not record data against targets you were not actually running.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    Always tell the supervising analyst the same day that you worked without the plan,
    even if the session went perfectly well.
  - If working without documents becomes normal where you are, raise it as a systems problem in writing.
