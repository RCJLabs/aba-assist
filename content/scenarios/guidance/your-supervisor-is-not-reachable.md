id: your-supervisor-is-not-reachable
kind: guidance
title: You need a decision and your supervisor is not answering
situation: >-
  Something has come up that is not in the plan, nobody is in danger, and you need
  direction. You have called and messaged your supervising analyst and heard nothing. The
  session is running now and the family is looking at you.
setting: any
audience: [RBT, BCaBA]
riskFlags: []
steps:
  - text: Decide first whether this actually has to be answered inside this session, or only before the next one.
    rationale: >-
      Most questions that feel urgent are not. A target you are unsure about can simply be
      left until you have direction, and the session continues around it.
  - text: Fall back to what is already written — the plan, the protocol, the last written instruction you were given.
    rationale: >-
      Following the written plan is never the wrong answer, and an existing instruction is
      direction you already have.
  - text: If nothing written covers it, do the safe, ordinary, unremarkable thing and change nothing about the program.
    rationale: >-
      Pausing a target costs a session of data. Improvising a new procedure costs the
      integrity of the whole comparison and may teach something nobody intended.
  - text: Use your organization's backup route — a second analyst, the clinical director, whoever the on-call arrangement names.
    rationale: >-
      Your employer is required to keep supervision available. Not being able to reach
      anybody is a scheduling problem for them to fix, not a gap for you to fill.
  - text: Write down the question, the time you asked it, and what you did instead, and send it before you leave.
    rationale: >-
      It closes the loop, and it creates the record that shows the question was raised at
      the time rather than reconstructed afterwards.
whatNotToDo:
  - Do not invent a new procedure and run it because somebody had to decide something.
  - Do not ask the family what they would like you to do instead, which puts a clinical decision on them.
  - Do not let it go unrecorded because it resolved itself.
whenToEscalate:
  - >-
    If you cannot reach any qualified supervisor at all and this is becoming a pattern,
    raise it with your employer in writing — practicing without supervision on record puts
    your own certification at risk.
  - Anything involving danger or injury is a different situation entirely and does not wait for a call back.
